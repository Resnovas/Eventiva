## Linear description

## Goal

A cashier with the right role turns any phone, tablet or desktop running Eventiva into a register for an event booth or box office, sells fast with cash or card, and keeps selling through a loss of connectivity and a device restart.

## Context

* Parent <issue id="7d939472-20ba-4512-b231-c0f04c05574c" label="EVE-107" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-107/f9-online-and-in-person-sales-storefront-point-of-sale-orders-payments" /> sections 2, 5, 6 and decision D3 (offline card) and D6 (offline capacity per line kind).
* Channels attach to an F1 Event and optionally a Phase and Space (<issue id="fbcff205-decd-464a-b8ab-a1278e71fbe4" label="EVE-73" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-73/f1-events-and-production-planning-the-event-record-run-of-show" />). Same app for every role; the register appears only with sales:register:operate.
* Card readers are F9.6; this slice delivers cash, the register itself and the offline queue, with card through the fake terminal.

## Scope

1. Channels screen: sales.channel.create, open, close for kinds register, box office and booth, including per-device daily and per-order offline card limits (default 0).
2. Register pairing (sales.register.pair, unpair) binding a device to a channel; register session open with opening float, cash in and out, close with counted cash and variance (sales:register_session_closed event to F11).
3. Register screen: product tile grid, search and barcode input (scanner as keyboard), cart panel, number pad, discounts, park and recall a sale, payment sheet (cash with change due, card via CardTerminal port, split tender).
4. Offline: catalogue, prices and tax snapshot cached on the device; orders, payments and cash movements written to local SQLite and queued; upload through [sales.order.place](<http://sales.order.place>) idempotently on reconnect; queue survives restart; connectivity and queue size shown on screen and in the projection.
5. Offline limit enforcement before taking an offline card payment; online-only line kinds blocked offline.
6. Age-restricted product prompt.
7. Revoked cashier: queued sales still upload but are flagged for review.

## Acceptance criteria

* Scripted outage test: 50 offline sales (cash and fake card) across a device restart upload once each on reconnect, in order, with correct session totals.
* Per-order and daily offline limits block the sale that would exceed them; typed error and clear message.
* Median single-line sale under 20 seconds in a timed manual run on Android, recorded here.
* Cash session close produces the variance and the event F11 consumes.
* Agent on the register can ring up a cash sale through the same actions and says that card confirmation waits for connection.

## Constraints
