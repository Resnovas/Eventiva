## Linear description

## Goal

An organiser invites vendors from the directory (F5) to sell at an event, either on the organiser's registers and store or on their own, and each sale pays the vendor its share automatically, minus the organiser's commission.

## Context

* Parent <issue id="7d939472-20ba-4512-b231-c0f04c05574c" label="EVE-107" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-107/f9-online-and-in-person-sales-storefront-point-of-sale-orders-payments" /> decision D5 (recommended: spec now, build after F9.3) and D2 (connected accounts make each tenant and vendor a merchant).
* Vendors are F5 directory listings linked to an F4 organisation; F9 does not redefine them.

## Scope

1. Vendor invitation to a channel with a SplitRule: commission percent or fixed per line, who is merchant of record for the vendor's lines (vendor recommended), payout timing.
2. Vendor role: external users limited to their own products and their own sales on channels they are invited to (<issue id="7f612e80-3332-4acf-a74c-d35db06fe4e8" label="EVE-43" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-43/p14-build-identity-and-access-orgs-authorization-user-profiles-and" /> scoped permissions).
3. Connected account onboarding for vendors through the PaymentGateway (approval always).
4. Mixed carts: lines from several vendors in one checkout; payment split per SplitRule at capture; refunds reverse the split proportionally.
5. Vendor dashboard: their sales, fees, commission and payouts.
6. Events for F11 carry vendor share and commission so the organiser books commission income only.
7. Behind `f9-vendor-splits` (default off).

## Directory quote payments (F5 contract, <issue id="7dba7530-e7b6-4259-be26-9924f9ebddcb" label="EVE-237" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-237/f514-build-quote-payments-with-commission-deducted-at-source" />)

Decided by Jonathan on 2026-11-08 in F5 (D5-6 b): organisers pay accepted directory quotes through Eventiva.

* The vendor tenant is merchant of record on its own connected account. Eventiva's commission is the platform fee on each capture, and Eventiva holds no balance.
* F5 creates one PaymentRequest per quote schedule line (deposit, balance) and calls [directory.payment.pay](<http://directory.payment.pay>). That opens an F9 checkout session through the PaymentGateway port (<issue id="382cab34-de6b-4524-8a47-241353c76b2b" label="EVE-174" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-174/f93-build-the-payment-gateway-port-and-online-card-payments" />) with destination = vendor connected account and application fee = commission. The commission base excludes VAT, the rate comes from the F5 CommissionPlan, and tests use 5 percent.
* F9 provides: capture, refund and dispute events on the change feed carrying the PaymentRequest id; refunds (<issue id="cc848306-1827-4682-a18a-e5c255970dd3" label="EVE-215" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-215/f98-build-refunds-cancellations-exchanges-and-disputes" />) that reverse the platform fee in proportion; and connected-account onboarding status per tenant, so F5 can block payment requests to vendors not yet onboarded.
* The monthly VAT invoice for commission comes from the operator tenant through F11 (<issue id="2ef1ed05-1c6a-4b1b-9b1c-04331398cac8" label="EVE-126" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-126/f114-build-sales-invoices-credit-notes-and-receivables" />), not from F9.

## Acceptance criteria

* A vendor cannot see another vendor's or the organiser's sales through any surface.
* Sandbox evidence with the real adapter once D2 and D5 are approved.
