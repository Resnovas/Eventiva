## Linear description

## Goal

Finance closes each month and year with a checklist, locks the books, and can move the whole ledger to the online finance platform with identical balances.

## Context

* Parent <issue id="158ba369-976e-47ea-9f86-a8df608cb4d0" label="EVE-69" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-69/f11-accounting-ledgers-invoices-bills-event-budgets-and-tax" /> sections 4, 11 and 12. House accounting rule: durable money data stays 100 percent compatible with the online role; exportable history that can be replayed; stable external references mapping 1:1.

## Scope

1. Close checklist per period: unreconciled bank lines, posting exceptions (F11.2), unapproved bills, draft journals, deferred revenue release; each item links to its screen.
2. `accounting.period.close` (lock date, approval always) and `reopen` (approval always, reason required, audited).
3. Year end: close income and expense to retained earnings with one generated journal; opening balances for the new year.
4. Ledger replay export: every balance and transaction with stable references, ISO-4217 currency and precision, in a versioned schema; import into an empty ledger through `LedgerPort`.
5. Id map table (local id to online id), reversible, created on first export.
6. Screen: period close with checklist; projection includes the checklist state.
7. Agent: runs the checklist and reports blockers; cannot close or reopen without approval.

## Acceptance criteria

* Posting into a closed period is rejected on every surface; reopening needs approval and writes an audit row with the reason.
* Year-end journal leaves income and expense accounts at zero and retained earnings correct on the fixture.
* Replay export from the Postgres ledger adapter imported into the in-memory adapter gives identical balances for every account and dimension (round-trip test).

## Constraints

Effect-TS v3; no new dependency; no online platform sign-up in this slice; flag `f11-accounting`; ship to main with `node --run check` results pasted here.

## Done signal
