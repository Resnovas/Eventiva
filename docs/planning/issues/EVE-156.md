## Linear description

## Goal

Before anyone works, HR records how their right to work was checked, keeps the evidence for exactly as long as the law needs, and is reminded before time-limited permission runs out; background checks for roles that need them are recorded the same way.

## Scope

1. Tables RightToWorkCheck (method: manual document, online share code, provider; checkedAt; checker; outcome; followUpDate; evidence fileIds) and BackgroundCheck (type, level, reference, issuedAt, recheckDate, outcome), both immutable; corrections are new rows.
2. Actions `hr.rightToWork.record` (confirm), `hr.rightToWork.get` (coordinators see only a status flag), `hr.rightToWork.followUps.list`, and `hr.backgroundChecks.record` / `.get` with the same masking.
3. Guided screen for a manual check: document types, the check steps, capture of copies, checker declaration (whitelabel, UK-first wording behind the jurisdiction port).
4. Retention: evidence kept for the engagement plus two years after it ends, then purged by the F13.2 purge run.
5. Follow-up reminders 60 and 28 days before followUpDate; a passed followUpDate with no new check sets status expired, which makes the worker ineligible in F13.5.
6. `IdentityCheck` and `BackgroundCheck` ports with only the manual adapter; no provider integration in this issue.

## Acceptance criteria

* A worker with no passed check is ineligible for any F2 role (reason no right to work).
* A time-limited check with follow-up in 30 days produces a reminder and becomes expired on the date if not renewed.
* A crew coordinator and their agent see only valid, expiring or expired, never documents or nationality (negative test per surface).
* Evidence files are purged two years after the engagement ends in a time-travel test.
