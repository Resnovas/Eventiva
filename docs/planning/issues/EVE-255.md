## BINDING owner decision — 15 November 2026: human-in-the-loop for ALL operational emergency actions

**Never automatically actuate a show-control or operational response from an incident, alarm, emergency sensor, issue classification, agent interpretation, integration alert or rules-engine event.** An alarm/event may be ingested, classified, displayed, logged, correlated, escalated to people, propose a configured SOP or preset, and notify designated staff; it must NOT automatically GO/hold/pause/mute audio, set venue lights/DMX, trigger PA/media/announcements, start emergency scene/preset, dispatch units or carry out other consequential incident-response operations. The possibility of false alarms and resulting public panic/harm is the rationale. This is an unconditional product policy for **operational incidents and alarms**, not an admin-configurable bypass or an exception for 'tested integration'. A permissioned, accountable **human must deliberately initiate** the approved action following assessment; log initiator, confirmation, linked incident, preset/version, result, and failures. Provide quick incident-linked actions but not default-on automatic acceptance.

**Differentiate ordinary show automation:** separately programmed and human-armed cue stacks, playback sequences, lighting console input, MIDI/OSC/MSC and timecode may continue to start permitted *non-emergency show cues* according to show programming; that is not authority for automatically activating an incident/emergency response. Incoming external signals claiming to be alarms are treated as alerts for human evaluation even if received over a show-control protocol. An external operator deliberately firing an authorized ordinary cue is still allowed, subject to arming and permissions.

**System-of-systems boundary:** independent certified fire alarms, evacuation/voice alarm, emergency lighting and physical overrides retain their own mandatory automatic functions. Eventiva's human-in-the-loop restriction is for Eventiva-initiated control actions and must not inhibit certified systems, lawful fire-panel interlocks, or physical emergency interventions. Do not call for an extra Eventiva confirmation where it could delay or prevent those independent measures. Any proposed direct control of safety-certified hardware requires separate safety engineering and approval.

**Acceptance criteria:** simulate a false fire/heat alarm and a mistakenly classified crowd incident: both notify MDT with a proposed response and initiate **zero** show/lighting/audio/PA/dispatch outputs without a deliberate authorized human action; no auto approval through agents, tenant rules or remote console input marked as emergency. Authorized manual response from MDT executes only the selected versioned preset and clearly reports partial success/failure. Ordinary human-armed playback and mapped external non-emergency cue input still work independently. Test no deferred/replayed emergency output after reconnect.

Implementation and dependencies remain unapproved.

## MDT and external command boundary — clarified 15 November 2026

See <issue id="e6838c83-005e-4ec5-bc2f-931de6cd0cb8" label="EVE-256" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-256/connect-mdt-incidents-and-external-controls-to-live-show-cues-and" />: a controller can request approved show actions and emergency response presets through the F7 MDT, but they execute only via the authorized F1 cue/command interface and local show engine. Incoming desk/console control must launch mapped native audio/video/lighting cues through the same arming/version/permission model. Support response per-endpoint status and partial failures, offline-local operations, and no stale/replayed emergency commands. The statement that an incoming *incident notification* does not itself fire emergency outputs remains correct; an **authorized deliberate MDT emergency-action command** may invoke a rehearsed preset. No automatic modification of published/armed show snapshots.

## Goal and decision status

**Owner-approved product scope, implementation NOT YET AUTHORIZED.** Deliver an independently deployable **local show playback / cue execution engine** for Eventiva, inspired by the full QLab 5 cue functionality in [EVE-254](<https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-254/define-qlab-class-cue-playback-and-end-to-end-production-control>), **not as a submodule of the F7 MDT/CAD**. Must work for a single-host Community deployment as well as paired production show machines, with essential live execution independent of WAN/remote AI.

## Functional cue types and control

* Audio playback (local files, trims, loop, fades, volume, multichannel outputs/patch/routing), live Mic input, video/still media, camera inputs, rendered text, lighting, fades/effects, network/OSC, MIDI/MIDI file/MSC, LTC/MTC timecode and programmable/script cues; treat advanced routing/codecs/effects as phased capabilities with exact per-platform requirements and tests.
* Lists with playhead & GO, nested/parallel and sequential group cues, timeline and cue carts for out-of-order execution, auto-follow and auto-continue, waits/delays/repeats, targeted start/stop/pause/load/reset, fade/devamp, goto/target, explicit cue arm/disarm, duration/timecode scheduling, logging and output-device status.
* Author cues, review/edit with live preview versus rehearsal/test versus operator show mode; no test action may unexpectedly actuate live output. Validated script semantics, secure sandbox/allowed actions and resource budgets (separate exact runtime/dependency approval; never run arbitrary scripts as system user).
* Incoming local hardware/control triggers (OSC, MIDI/MSC, LTC/MTC and selected network input) as well as outbound ports; map through signed/authorized show-run commands with input validation, rate/replay handling, per-cue trigger permission, clock-source attribution and output fencing. Distinguish external transport triggers from incoming **operational issue/incident** notifications owned by F7.
* Versioned asset/preload/manifest catalog, missing-file/codec test, media preflight, supported OS drivers/codecs/displays and output patch. Keep console/media-server/QLab interoperability through <issue id="96b947e4-5f8f-4905-9e99-fa55a95a0482" label="EVE-231" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-231/f113-build-the-show-bridge-a-local-process-that-fires-cues-to-consoles" />'s transport services, not a competing command implementation.

## Easy reuse / repeated shows

* A **ShowTemplate** and reusable, versioned **CueSet** can include cue-list, cart, grouped sequences and dependent assets; support one-click create/recreate of show runs for repeated dates, productions, tours or venues with day-zero-relative timings, scene/cue overrides, media links and venue-specific output/patch profiles.
* Instantiate a **published pinned snapshot** for each running show, preserving exact cue-set versions/media hashes, operator-authorized changes, test evidence and a frozen output manifest. Source template edits cannot silently modify a live or published run. Explain/present diffs and allow a deliberate reviewed update/rehearsal workflow.
* Shared Event/Phase/Space/RunOfShow from F1, F6 equipment/patch, F3 build tasks, F7 incident/MDT work and <issue id="5beffe53-7de1-44a4-9d23-3166327dc2b1" label="EVE-253" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-253/build-shared-guided-sop-procedures-and-governed-step-execution" /> SOP integration by IDs. Avoid creating a duplicate Event, RunOfShow, task, incident or workflow engine.

## Runtime / safety architecture

* Local deterministic clock and cue scheduler with independent output drivers; Effect Cluster/Workflow/ActionHost handles durable publication, roles, audit and coordination, **never per-frame media processing** or arbitrary offline live commands. A local operator remains authoritative on-site if WAN fails, with bounded signed authorization and exact version/readiness checks; design offline live authorization separately rather than bypassing security.
* Fencing/leadership prevents multiple devices simultaneously issuing GO or repeated non-idempotent MIDI/network effects during failover; distinguish accepted command, transport delivered and external device-observed success. Expose stale/unknown physical state explicitly.
* Human-controlled arming, manual takeover, configurable emergency safe/stop behaviors with redundant independent physical safety equipment; no software substitutes for certified evacuation/fire/emergency functions. Actions require current permissions and controlled approvals; do not let agents approve themselves or call arbitrary scripts.
* Export reliable logs and after-action records; controlled closure/derig workflow links F1/F3/F6/F7 actual outcomes into one Eventiva production lifecycle.

## Acceptance cases to define before implementation

* Load/publish/reuse a cue set with multiple nested groups, cue carts, fades and several cue types for three dates/venues; show instances remain unchanged when the template is edited until explicitly updated.
* Receive real OSC/MIDI/timecode triggers and emit the corresponding paired outputs locally at measured latency/jitter; show what happens for replayed, out-of-order or unauthorized triggers.
* Run simultaneous audio/video output and a lighting cue while WAN is offline; preloading/asset validation and a local clock still operate. Driver/codec/platform-specific performance measured on actual supported hardware, not estimated.
* Demonstrate rehearsal and preview have no unintended live outputs, disarmed invocation fails, and local manual STOP still works when the server/agent is unreachable. Tests for crash, clock jump, endpoint missing, operator handover and split-brain.
* An incoming F7 operational incident is visible and links to the active show and relevant approved SOP without automatically firing emergency outputs.
* Agent-native inventory and UI-to-agent screen parity; no arbitrary script, unsafe provider privilege escalation or forbidden action route.
