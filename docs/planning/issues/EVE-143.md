## Linear description

## Goal

Every vehicle has its services planned by date or mileage, repairs run as work orders with an internal mechanic or an external garage, fuel and mileage are logged from the phone, and finance can see what each vehicle and each event cost.

## Context

* Parent: [EVE-71](<https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-71>) actions `fleet.service.schedule`, `fleet.workOrder.*`, `fleet.fuel.log`, `fleet.odometer.record`, `fleet.costs.exportForEvent`; entities ServiceSchedule, WorkOrder, FuelEntry, OdometerReading; screen Maintenance board.
* Parts used are F6 Products ([EVE-81](<https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-81>)); consuming them is `inventory.stock.move` from the warehouse to the virtual consumption location with a ref to the work order (F6 spec [EVE-63](<https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-63/f6-warehousing-and-inventory-equipment-stock-availability-purchasing>) models vehicles as stock locations; parts consumption is a stock movement owned by F6). Garages are F4 Organisations. Costs leave as F11 Cost lines.
* Due reminders run as durable scheduled work ([EVE-54](<https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-54>)).

## Scope

1. ServiceSchedule per vehicle or vehicle type: interval by days and/or distance, next due computed from the last completed service and latest odometer.
2. WorkOrder: tasks, assigned mechanic or garage, parts (F6 Product ids and quantities; completing the order calls `inventory.stock.move`), labour, cost, attachments; states open, in progress, waiting parts, complete (terminal). Opening from a Defect (F7.4) links them; completing closes the defect after confirm.
3. FuelEntry and OdometerReading: offline append-only from the phone, receipt photo, litres or kWh, cost; lower odometer flagged.
4. `fleet.costs.exportForEvent`: fuel, work orders and hire costs for vehicles booked on an F1 Event, as F11 Cost lines; until F11 exists, a CSV export.
5. Maintenance board (Kanban by state) and a Vehicle detail maintenance tab; garage external view sees only its work orders.
6. Durable job: daily check that emits "service due in 14 days or 500 km" suggestions (consumed by F7.12).

## Acceptance criteria

* Next due recalculates after a completed service and after a new odometer reading (tests for date-only, distance-only and both, whichever first).
* A garage user cannot see vehicles or work orders not assigned to it.
* Cost export for a sample event totals fuel, repair and hire lines correctly against a fixture.
* The due-check job survives a runner restart without duplicate suggestions (idempotent by vehicle and due date).

## Constraints

Effect-TS v3 house rules; no zod; Rambo-UI; behind `eventiva-fleet`; money as integer minor units with currency; no new dependency without Jonathan approving it on this issue; tests with `@effect/vitest`; docs; ships to main per the AGENTS.md delivery exception with `node --run check` results pasted here.
