## Linear description

## Goal

A stage manager calls the show from Eventiva: starts segments, sees over and under run live, stands by and goes cues, and every device on the team follows the same clock, with a full log afterwards.

## Context

* Parent: <issue id="fbcff205-decd-464a-b8ab-a1278e71fbe4" label="EVE-73" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-73/f1-events-and-production-planning-the-event-record-run-of-show" /> sections 4 (`show.*`, `sequence.*`), 5 (ShowRun, ShowRunEntry, ShowSequence, live behaviour without internet), 6 (Show caller, Show sequence), 7 (approvals), decision D3.
* This slice works without any hardware: cues are called by voice from the screen. Sending cues to devices is F1.13. Live data is never queued offline (<issue id="fbcff205-decd-464a-b8ab-a1278e71fbe4" label="EVE-73" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-73/f1-events-and-production-planning-the-event-record-run-of-show" /> section 5).
* The live clock lives on the show bridge when one is paired (F1.13); without a bridge, the caller's device is the clock and others follow over the live channel (<issue id="0ab161c4-ed9c-4581-86fe-91fa1f4216aa" label="EVE-46" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-46/p17-build-the-http-api-streaming-and-the-typed-client-from-the-action" /> streaming).

## Scope

1. Tables `show_run` (run_of_show_id, started_by, started_at, ended_at, armed_by, clock source) and `show_run_entry` (at, kind: segment_go, cue_standby, cue_fire, cue_result, adjust, stop; actor person or agent; outcome) and `show_sequence` (event_id, timecode format 24, 25, 29.97 drop frame, 30; start offset; cues placed at timecode).
2. Actions: `show.start`, `show.goSegment`, `show.adjust` (add or remove time, re-plan the rest), `show.end`, `cue.standby`, `sequence.create`, `sequence.placeCue`, `sequence.preview` (plays the timeline on screen only, no output).
3. Live drift: pure function from planned times (F1.3 timing engine) and actual go times to projected end and per-segment over or under.
4. Live channel: followers subscribe to a show run stream; if the stream drops they keep counting locally, mark timings stale and stop showing standby as current.
5. Show log: every entry with actor and time; exported as CSV after the show; synced when back online.
6. Agent: can be delegated a show run by a person, after which it may `show.goSegment` and `cue.standby` on schedule; the delegating person can take it back at once. `show.arm` and firing rules are in F1.13.
7. Screens: Show caller (big clock, current and next, drift, cue stack, keyboard shortcuts with large targets, never colour alone) and Show sequence (timeline with timecode ruler, playhead, preview) with projections and bound controls.

## Acceptance criteria

* Drift tests: segment runs 2 minutes over, the projected end moves by 2 minutes; `show.adjust` minus 2 recovers it.
* Three followers stay within 100 ms of the caller's clock on a LAN; one follower losing the stream shows stale timing within 2 seconds.
* With the internet off but the LAN up, a show run completes and its log syncs when the internet returns.
* Timecode conversion tests for every supported frame rate including 29.97 drop frame.
* A delegated agent goes segments on time in a simulated run, and a person taking control back stops it within one tick.
* Both screens pass the unbound-control check and axe, and the caller screen is fully usable by keyboard.

## Constraints

Effect-TS v3 house rules (drift and timecode maths are pure TypeScript; the live channel is an Effect stream); every operation is an inventory action with one permission check, audit and change record; UI from Rambo-UI, WCAG 2.2 AA; behind PostHog flag `f1-show-control`; no new dependency without Jonathan approving it on this issue; whitelabel ASCII copy; people docs and ai-docs in the same commit; ships to main with `node --run check` results here.
