## Goal

Emails and meetings with known contacts appear on their timeline without anyone logging them, and follow-ups can be sent from the user's own mailbox.

## Scope

1. Neutral ports `MailboxSync` (list since cursor, fetch message, send) and `CalendarSync` (list events since cursor) as Effect services in `integrations/crm-mail`; domain code depends only on the ports.
2. No-vendor first step: a per-tenant forwarding address (through the <issue id="9aae6a50-4a43-4327-ae6a-51d18bc69b35" label="EVE-24" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-24/port-notifications-messaging-channels-and-transactional-email" /> inbound path) that turns forwarded or BCC'd mail into Interactions for matched parties.
3. Adapters, after approval: Google (Gmail and Calendar APIs) and Microsoft (Graph mail and calendar), OAuth with least scopes (read metadata and bodies, send), per member.
4. Actions `crm.mailbox.connect` (confirm) / `disconnect` / `sync` / `status`; matching rules (only messages with at least one known party are stored; internal-only threads skipped; per-member exclusion list).
5. Sync job: incremental by cursor, idempotent by external message id, backoff on provider limits, revocation stops the job at the next step.
6. Send: `crm.followUp.send` drafts by agent or person, sends through the member's mailbox after confirm, logged as an outbound Interaction; marketing sends refuse without consent (F4.8 check).
7. Screen: Mailbox connections in settings with status and last sync. Flag `crm-mailbox-sync`.

## Acceptance criteria

* Tests with fake port implementations: duplicate delivery stores one Interaction; an unknown sender is not stored; revoking a connection stops a running sync; a token never appears in logs, spans or the audit payload.
* With approved adapters: a real test mailbox syncs 100 messages and 10 meetings into the right timelines (evidence pasted here).
* `node --run check` green.
