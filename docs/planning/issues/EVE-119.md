## Goal

An organiser sends one brief to several suppliers, and each supplier gets a private thread with that organiser that nobody else can see.

## Context

* <issue id="b22fbb0d-100b-47a3-8c32-eeacadc4c9fe" label="EVE-66" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-66/f5-directory-and-marketplace-vendor-venue-and-professional-listings" /> sections 4, 5 (Enquiry, EnquiryRecipient, Message) and 9 (cross-tenant rule).
* This is the first record type shared between two tenants. <issue id="7f612e80-3332-4acf-a74c-d35db06fe4e8" label="EVE-43" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-43/p14-build-identity-and-access-orgs-authorization-user-profiles-and" /> isolates tenants; this issue adds a two-party authorization rule there: the principal belongs to the organiser tenant or the vendor tenant of the EnquiryRecipient row and holds the enquiry permission in that tenant. It must be reviewed with <issue id="7f612e80-3332-4acf-a74c-d35db06fe4e8" label="EVE-43" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-43/p14-build-identity-and-access-orgs-authorization-user-profiles-and" />'s owner before code.
* Notifications go through <issue id="9aae6a50-4a43-4327-ae6a-51d18bc69b35" label="EVE-24" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-24/port-notifications-messaging-channels-and-transactional-email" />; the vendor inbox updates from the change feed (<issue id="ee08644c-eb3c-4a46-aea7-745864ad4263" label="EVE-45" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-45/p16-build-the-change-flow-domain-events-and-a-durable-resource-change" />).

## Scope

 1. Enquiry (organiser tenant), EnquiryRecipient (both tenant ids), Message (append-only) tables.
 2. Actions `directory.enquiry.draft`, `.send`, `.withdraw`, `.message`, `.decline`. Approvals: send is confirm, always when more than 5 recipients; message is confirm when the caller is an agent.
 3. The brief: dates, headcount, location, budget range (optional, visible to recipients only if the organiser opts in), requirements text, attachments ([EVE-25](<https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-25/port-files-private-attachments-artifacts-and-document-import>)), optional F1 Event id. Prefill from the F1 Event when given.
 4. Recipient privacy: recipients never see each other's identity or messages; the organiser sees all threads.
 5. EnquiryRecipient state machine: new, viewed, responded, declined, quoted, won, lost, expired; enquiries auto-expire after a configurable period (default 30 days, configuration via [EVE-41](<https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-41/p12-build-app-config-settings-secrets-and-credentials>)).
 6. Screens: enquiry composer (organiser), enquiry inbox and thread (vendor), enquiry overview with per-recipient status (organiser). Projections mark other-tenant text as untrusted for the agent.
 7. Offline: drafts and messages queue offline and are authorized on reconnect; the UI shows queued, never sent.
 8. Anti-spam: per-tenant daily send limit, and a listing can opt out of enquiries from unverified organisers.
 9. Response-time statistic per listing computed from first vendor response.
10. CRM capture: on send, call crm.enquiry.capture (<issue id="aa5aaa4a-23e6-4982-bde3-b771cf4bf000" label="EVE-137" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-137/f46-capture-enquiries-and-track-opportunities-through-a-pipeline-into" />) in the vendor tenant through the action inventory (acting as the system for that tenant, audited) and store the returned id as crmEnquiryRef; the organiser side links the supplier to an F4 Organisation (find or create). If F4 is not built yet, record the capture as pending and replay it when it ships.
11. Unclaimed recipients: a recipient listing that is unclaimed gets status `awaiting claim`; F5.13 (<issue id="0c66a703-79d5-407a-a0c0-69ab5efdc31b" label="EVE-236" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-236/f513-build-seeded-listings-claiming-claim-disputes-and-removal" />) sends the claim invitation, and the recipient becomes a normal thread when the listing is claimed. The organiser sees the unclaimed label in the composer before sending.

## Acceptance criteria

* Negative test matrix: recipient A cannot read recipient B's thread; vendor cannot list the organiser's other enquiries; organiser member without enquiry:read cannot read threads; agent for a viewer cannot send.
* A queued offline message is sent once on reconnect (dedupe test) and an enquiry withdrawn meanwhile rejects it with a typed error.
* An instruction inside a vendor message is not executed by the agent (agent eval fixture via <issue id="bef04957-8bec-479e-85ee-5880dfc9fc8c" label="EVE-29" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-29/port-agent-evaluations-linked-to-posthog-datasets" /> when available, unit test of the untrusted marker now).
* Audit rows on both tenants for every send.

### D5-12 coordination (2026-11-13) - selective supplier enquiries and varied procurement workflows

* F5.7 must support one or multiple **individually selected** suppliers in an organiser-initiated approved batch, not assume every invited supplier gets each revision. A single authorised bulk action can release a batch, but maintain distinct per-supplier version-locked request snapshots, recipients, approval records, send status and idempotent retry, with no cross-supplier price/brief leakage.
* Suppliers and sourcing packages may use different enquiry workflows: prescriptive item/quantity RFQ, supplier-designed/specification-led proposal (e.g. video wall), alternatives, or combined multi-discipline scope (e.g. video + audio). The same requirement may have multiple candidate suppliers; one supplier may address many requirements/categories in one response. Preserve mappings and separate supplier-visible brief versions from exploratory plan revisions.
* Organisers can pause/skip a supplier once a preferred provider emerges, without deleting quote/RFP history or forcing re-contact. Changes to grouping or internal comparison views must never send supplier communications.
* Pricing/document response and grouping ownership stays in <issue id="f08bafe4-f32d-4906-9e87-5d69df56d6a0" label="EVE-124" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-124/f58-build-quotes-quote-comparison-and-the-acceptance-handoff-to-the" />. Original supplier proposal is immutable; F5.7 sends and tracks authorised briefs and private threads, not inferred partial bundle prices. Follow D5-11's manual release gate and <issue id="5c7aeec2-d49c-4028-9eca-1bc641d3618c" label="EVE-13" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-13/establish-modular-core-foundations-for-database-adapters-expo-and" /> shared agent-native actions.

### Planning decision - convey exact/functional scope and allow supplier alternatives (2026-11-13; D5-13 in <issue id="f08bafe4-f32d-4906-9e87-5d69df56d6a0" label="EVE-124" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-124/f58-build-quotes-quote-comparison-and-the-acceptance-handoff-to-the" />)

* Version-locked supplier enquiry briefs expose buyer's per-requirement policy: 'Exact item required' with exact manufacturer/model/variant and rider/source constraints, or 'Function-based - recommend suitable product' with intended use, hard/soft criteria, quantity and any exclusions. Never collapse requirements into unqualified free text or assume all briefs require a manufacturer SKU.
* Supplier responses may provide a named proposed product, specification and evidence linked to a requirement ID, one or several options, or a design-led solution. For exact requirements, alternate proposals are explicitly 'deviation requiring approval', separate from a compliant exact quotation. Capture omissions, exceptions, accessories and price terms without changing the originating event requirement.
* Do not contact suppliers automatically when policies, requirement criteria or selected alternatives change internally: use D5-11's manual reviewed consolidated release gate; supplier-specific request baselines, isolation and multiple-supplier selection from D5-12 still apply.

### Planning coordination D5-14 - supplier selection/import at quoting (2026-11-13)

Suppliers may answer an RFQ by selecting a known model/kit from F6 and linking their own stock SKU, or by uploading a product specification/datasheet and submitting a new product for controlled intake. Quote can proceed with incompletely verified specifications, flagged for review rather than falsely equivalenced. Preserve original quote, supplier-selected model and evidence version; never replace it retroactively when catalogue records change. Cross-tenant availability is a consented, freshness-labelled projection, not raw F6 stock. No automatic external supplier messages based on inventory changes.
