## Linear description

## Goal

The sound team keeps the input list, monitor mixes and stage plot for each act on the event, built from riders, shared with the act and exported for the desk.

## Context

* Parent: <issue id="fbcff205-decd-464a-b8ab-a1278e71fbe4" label="EVE-73" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-73/f1-events-and-production-planning-the-event-record-run-of-show" /> sections 4, 5 (AudioChannel, MonitorMix), 6 (Audio screen).
* Riders and their extracted requirements come from F1.2. Speakers and stage boxes placed on the plan are DesignElements from F1.6. RF frequency coordination is out of scope (<issue id="fbcff205-decd-464a-b8ab-a1278e71fbe4" label="EVE-73" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-73/f1-events-and-production-planning-the-event-record-run-of-show" /> section 13).

## Scope

1. Tables `audio_channel` (event_id, optional performer contact_ref, optional segment_id, number, source, mic or DI, stand, stage box and port, desk patch, phantom power flag, notes) and `monitor_mix` (name, recipient, kind: wedge or in-ear, channels with levels as notes).
2. Actions: `audio.addChannel`, `audio.updateChannel`, `audio.moveChannel`, `audio.removeChannel`, `audio.addMix`, `audio.updateMix`, `audio.importFromRider` (from the F1.2 suggestion list, applied on confirmation).
3. Stage plot as a view of the plan canvas filtered to the sound and staging layers for one Space (no separate drawing tool).
4. Exports: input list CSV and PDF through `ConsoleExport` and <issue id="8d8d301b-d679-4dac-95bb-c24889179cd0" label="EVE-25" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-25/port-files-private-attachments-artifacts-and-document-import" />.
5. Sharing: an act sees and can comment on its own input list and mixes; edit only if granted.
6. Agent: builds an input list from a rider, flags duplicate stage box ports and channels with no source.
7. Screen: Audio (input list grid, mixes, stage plot view) with projection and bound controls.

## Acceptance criteria

* Importing a sample rider produces an input list the sound head confirms; duplicates are flagged.
* Two offline reorderings of channels converge in a stable order.
* The act sees only its own channels and mixes.
* CSV and PDF exports match snapshots.
* Screen passes the unbound-control check and axe.
