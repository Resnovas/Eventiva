Decision D5-1 (b) chosen by Jonathan on 2026-11-08: the directory also holds unclaimed listings seeded by Eventiva or organisers, which owners claim later.

## Goal

The directory has useful supply from day one, because Eventiva staff and organisers can add businesses that are not on Eventiva yet, and those businesses can claim, correct or remove their listing.

## Context

* <issue id="b22fbb0d-100b-47a3-8c32-eeacadc4c9fe" label="EVE-66" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-66/f5-directory-and-marketplace-vendor-venue-and-professional-listings" /> sections 4, 5, 9 and 11 (updated for seeded listings).
* An unclaimed listing has no accountable owner, so it carries only public business facts, is clearly labelled, and cannot take quotes, reviews or availability until claimed.
* Claiming transfers the same Listing id to the claimant tenant. F1 Event.venue_ref and F1 Space.venue_room_ref (<issue id="fbcff205-decd-464a-b8ab-a1278e71fbe4" label="EVE-73" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-73/f1-events-and-production-planning-the-event-record-run-of-show" />) stay valid across the transfer.
* Businesses only. Professionals are individuals, so they are never seeded (UK GDPR; they publish their own profile with consent through F5.3).

## Scope

1. Listing fields: ownership (claimed, unclaimed), seededBy (platform or organiser tenant id), seedSource (manual entry, public website given by the seeder), lastCheckedAt. Unclaimed listings live in a platform directory tenant scope.
2. Allowed fields on an unclaimed listing: name, kind (vendor or venue only), categories, address and point, website, public business phone and email, venue rooms with capacities from the venue's own published material. No media except a logo or photo the business published itself with a source link, no offerings or prices, no reviews, no availability.
3. Actions:
   * `directory.seed.create` and `.update`: platform:seed (Eventiva staff), or an organiser with enquiry:write. Organiser seeds go to moderation (F5.3) before they are public; the organiser can use them privately straight away.
   * `directory.claim.request`: a signed-in tenant asks to claim, with evidence. Automatic path: an email confirmation sent to an address on the listing's website domain (`directory.claim.verifyDomain`). Manual path: documents for a moderator.
   * `directory.claim.decide` (platform:moderate): approve transfers ownership, sets organisationRef to the claimant's F4 Organisation, and moves the listing to pending verification (F5.3 still applies before it shows as a claimed listing); reject with a statement of reasons.
   * `directory.claim.dispute`: another tenant contests a claim or a completed transfer once; a second moderator decides; the transfer and reversal are audited.
   * `directory.seed.removalRequest`: anyone (anonymous with the HumanCheck port) asks to remove or correct an unclaimed listing; removal for a business that asks is honoured within 30 days and its name goes on a do-not-seed list.
4. Enquiries to an unclaimed listing (F5.7): the organiser is told it is unclaimed; Eventiva sends one claim invitation email through <issue id="9aae6a50-4a43-4327-ae6a-51d18bc69b35" label="EVE-24" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-24/port-notifications-messaging-channels-and-transactional-email" /> to the listing's public business email, carrying the brief summary without the organiser's contact details, with an unsubscribe that adds the address to a suppression list (PECR soft rules for business addresses). The enquiry recipient stays in `awaiting claim` until claimed, then becomes a normal recipient. At most 3 invitations per listing per 30 days.
5. Staleness: an unclaimed listing not refreshed or claimed for 12 months is unpublished automatically; the seeder is told.
6. Public label "Unclaimed listing: details from public sources, not checked by the business" and a "Is this your business?" claim entry point on listing detail (F5.5).
7. Ranking: an unclaimed listing ranks below a claimed one at equal relevance; this is listed on the ranking parameters page (F5.4).
8. No scraping and no bulk import from third-party directories (terms and database right). A licensed bulk dataset needs Jonathan's approval on this issue first.
9. Sub-flag `directory-seeded-listings`, default off.

## Acceptance criteria

* An unclaimed listing rejects offering, price, review and availability writes with typed errors.
* Domain-email claim: a confirmation from an address on the listing's domain approves the claim end to end in a test; a mismatched domain falls back to manual review.
* After transfer the Listing id is unchanged and an F1 Event referencing it still resolves (integration test with a fixture Event).
* A dispute reverses a transfer when decided against the first claimant, with audit rows for both moves.
* A removal request removes the listing and blocks re-seeding of the same name and domain (test).
* Invitation emails respect the per-listing cap and the suppression list (test).
