## Linear description

## Goal

Finance can see what the equipment and stock are worth at any date, and every period's value changes reach accounting once, balanced against the movement ledger.

## Context

* Parent <issue id="928dc098-5cc9-4586-88bb-50abbe03071a" label="EVE-63" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-63/f6-warehousing-and-inventory-equipment-stock-availability-purchasing" /> decision D6 (recommended): cost per serialised asset with straight-line depreciation; average cost for bulk and consumables. Boundary agreed with the F11 thread (<issue id="158ba369-976e-47ea-9f86-a8df608cb4d0" label="EVE-69" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-69/f11-accounting-ledgers-invoices-bills-event-budgets-and-tax" />) 2026-11-01: goods received are emitted per receipt by F6.8; this issue covers the per-period export.
* Amounts in integer minor units with currency; F11 tax and currency settings.

## Scope

1. Depreciation settings per product category (useful life months, residual percent) in <issue id="9d08c369-bac9-4eb7-b8a7-329b5ec9bff0" label="EVE-41" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-41/p12-build-app-config-settings-secrets-and-credentials" /> settings; per-asset override.
2. Average cost per bulk and consumable product per org, updated on each receipt; consumption and write-offs valued at the average at the time.
3. Actions `inventory.valuation.report` (as-of date, by category, location, method) and `inventory.valuation.exportToAccounts` (period; confirm; idempotent per period; emits `inventory:valuation_exported` on the change feed with lines for depreciation, average-cost consumption, asset additions and retirements, and write-offs from lost or scrapped items).
4. A reconciliation check: the export's quantity totals equal the movement ledger for the period, else the export fails with `ValuationOutOfBalance`.
5. Screen Valuation (report table and chart) with <issue id="91297309-acc4-4bb9-ad68-33fbc2720e5a" label="EVE-50" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-50/p111-build-screen-awareness-app-state-navigation-and-the-screen" /> projection; visible only with `inventory:valuation:read`.

## Acceptance criteria

* An asset costing 120000 minor units with a 60-month life and 0 residual depreciates 2000 per month; retiring it in month 13 exports a retirement with net book value 96000.
* Exporting the same period twice produces one event.
* A deliberately inserted unbalanced test movement makes the export fail with `ValuationOutOfBalance`.
* The agent can produce the report for a person with finance access and is refused for warehouse crew.
