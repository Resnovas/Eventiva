## Linear description

## Goal

No asset goes out unfit for use: inspections and services are scheduled, recorded with evidence, and enforced at check-out, and only qualified people can sign them off.

## Context

UK first, as per-jurisdiction configuration in <issue id="9d08c369-bac9-4eb7-b8a7-329b5ec9bff0" label="EVE-41" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-41/p12-build-app-config-settings-secrets-and-credentials" /> settings: lifting equipment thorough examination under LOLER 1998 (6 months for equipment lifting people and for lifting accessories, 12 months for other lifting equipment), work equipment under PUWER 1998, portable electrical in-service inspection and testing (IET Code of Practice). * Certificates are files (<issue id="8d8d301b-d679-4dac-95bb-c24889179cd0" label="EVE-25" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-25/port-files-private-attachments-artifacts-and-document-import" />). Vehicle maintenance is F7, not here.

## Scope

1. Tables `inventory_inspection_type` (code, name, interval, jurisdiction, blocks check-out when overdue yes or no), `inventory_inspection` (asset, type, result pass or fail, inspector person, evidence files, notes, next due; append-only) and `inventory_maintenance_job` (asset, kind service or repair, due, status, parts used as consumable movements, cost).
2. Actions `inventory.maintenance.schedule`, `log`, `inventory.inspection.record`, plus a read `inventory.inspection.due` (assets due or overdue in a window, optionally only those reserved).
3. Enforcement: a failed inspection quarantines the asset (F6.6); an overdue blocking inspection makes `scanOut` refuse with `AssetNotFitForUse`; release after a fail requires a later pass by a person holding `inventory:inspection:release`. The agent can never record a pass or release.
4. Seed UK inspection types (lifting people, lifting accessory, lifting other, portable electrical) with sources cited.
5. Screen Maintenance (calendar, list, inspection form) with <issue id="91297309-acc4-4bb9-ad68-33fbc2720e5a" label="EVE-50" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-50/p111-build-screen-awareness-app-state-navigation-and-the-screen" /> projection and bound controls.

## Acceptance criteria

* A chain hoist with a lifting examination 1 day overdue is refused at scan-out, online and offline.
* A failed electrical test quarantines the asset; a later pass by a qualified person releases it; the agent's attempt to record a pass is refused with `PermissionDenied`.
* `inspection.due` for next month lists every asset due, flags the ones reserved for events, and the agent offers to schedule them before each out date.
* Intervals and blocking are configurable per jurisdiction without code changes.
