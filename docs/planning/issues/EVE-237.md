## Goal

An organiser pays the vendor for an accepted quote inside Eventiva, the vendor receives the payment less Eventiva's agreed commission automatically, and Eventiva never holds the money itself.

## Scope

 1. CommissionPlan (platform scope, versioned): rate in basis points, optional minimum and cap per engagement, categories, effective from. Default plan plus optional per-tenant plans. The rate lives in this record, not in an environment variable; tests use 5 percent.
 2. CommissionTerms: `directory.commission.acceptTerms` (listing:publish, confirm) records plan version, who and when; plain-language terms, 15 days notice before a change applies (P2B).
 3. Payment schedule on the quote: the vendor sets it in the quote editor (F5.8): deposit percent or amount with due date, balance due date, or single payment. Shown to the organiser before acceptance.
 4. PaymentRequest per schedule line, created by the F5.8 handoff on acceptance: amount, due date, status (due, paid, overdue, refunded, cancelled). `directory.payment.pay` (quote:accept on the organiser side, always approval) opens F9 checkout against the vendor's connected account with a SplitRule of the platform commission. Card and bank transfer methods as F9.3 supports them.
 5. CommissionCharge per captured payment: payment id, base, rate, amount, plan version, status (collected, refunded, adjusted). Created from the gateway capture event, idempotent per payment.
 6. Refunds and cancellations: through F9.8; commission is refunded in proportion to the refunded amount unless the plan says commission on the deposit is non-refundable (default refundable). A dispute or chargeback on a payment reverses the commission share the same way.
 7. Off-platform settlement: if the organiser and vendor agree to settle outside Eventiva, the vendor records it with `directory.payment.markSettledOffPlatform` (reason, organiser confirmation on the thread); no commission is charged on it, the count shows in platform reporting only, and messages are never scanned for off-platform deals.
 8. Monthly commission statement and VAT invoice to each vendor for the commission collected (`directory.commission.statement`, listing:read), issued from the platform operator's own tenant with F11 sales invoices (<issue id="2ef1ed05-1c6a-4b1b-9b1c-04331398cac8" label="EVE-126" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-126/f114-build-sales-invoices-credit-notes-and-receivables" />) and VAT codes (<issue id="2dd28829-f5f7-4a45-bbf6-42592282e675" label="EVE-158" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-158/f117-build-vat-codes-vat-returns-and-mtd-submission" />), marked as already paid by deduction.
 9. Reminders: due and overdue payment notices to the organiser through <issue id="9aae6a50-4a43-4327-ae6a-51d18bc69b35" label="EVE-24" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-24/port-notifications-messaging-channels-and-transactional-email" />; the agent can explain a payment or commission line and prepare a payment, but `directory.payment.pay` always waits for the organiser's approval.
10. Screens: organiser payments panel on the quote and the F1 Event (schedule, due, paid); vendor payments and commission screen (payouts, commission, statements). Organisers see what they pay, never the vendor's commission.
11. Platform finance role `platform:commission`: set plans, approve adjustments, write off.
12. Telemetry: directory_payment_requested, directory_payment_paid, directory_payment_refunded, directory_commission_collected, directory_payment_settled_off_platform (amount buckets only).
13. Sub-flag `directory-payments`, default off.

## Acceptance criteria

* In the fake gateway, a deposit and a balance payment on one accepted quote each pay the vendor's connected account less the plan commission, with one CommissionCharge per capture even when the capture event is delivered twice.
* A 50 percent refund of the balance returns 50 percent of that payment's commission; a chargeback reverses the full share.
* A vendor without a connected account, verification or accepted terms cannot be paid; the organiser sees a clear reason and the quote can still be accepted.
* An organiser principal cannot read commission records (negative test); an agent cannot complete `directory.payment.pay` without approval on screen and through MCP.
* Ranking does not use commission or payment data (property test over F5.4 ranking inputs).
* Sandbox evidence with the real adapter once <issue id="382cab34-de6b-4524-8a47-241353c76b2b" label="EVE-174" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-174/f93-build-the-payment-gateway-port-and-online-card-payments" /> has an approved provider.
