## Goal

Build a first-class, tenant-customisable Eventiva learning and competency platform. Every deployment can deliver onboarding, operational procedures, compliance and product training through text, slides, video, assessments and practical evidence, online or offline.

## Core learning model

* Programmes → pathways → courses → modules → lessons/activities → assessments.
* Content blocks for rich text, slides/presentations, images, documents, audio, video with captions/transcripts, embedded simulations, interactive scenarios, checklists and supervised practical tasks.
* End-of-module, section and course assessments: question banks, randomisation, multiple choice/select, ordering, matching, scenario decisions, short answer, file/evidence upload and practical assessor sign-off.
* Configurable pass marks, attempt limits, feedback timing, remediation, prerequisites, expiry/renewal and recertification.
* Assign training to people, roles, teams, organisations, deployments, modules, events, sites and qualifications. ## Content and standards

* Ship default Eventiva product, security, privacy, agent, offline/degraded-mode and administrator training.
* Ship source-backed UK operational packs where reuse permits: JESIP principles and M/ETHANE, MDT/status/dispatch concepts, communication/radio procedure, incident classification and relevant event-operations workflows.
* Agency/jurisdiction profiles remain versioned and attributable; do not claim official accreditation, endorsement or live emergency-service suitability without authority.
* Tenants can clone, customise, translate and extend default material while preserving provenance and a diff from the source pack.
* AI may draft, translate, adapt and generate quizzes/simulations, but a named competent human approves regulated/safety content and answer keys before publication.

## Experience

* Responsive shared web/mobile/desktop experience, offline downloads and progress sync.
* WCAG/accessibility from inception: keyboard/screen-reader support, captions, transcripts, audio description metadata, reduced motion, accessible assessments and alternative formats.
* en-GB canonical source, module-namespaced i18n and terminology profiles.
* Learner dashboard, due/overdue/expiring training, bookmarks/notes, resume, notifications and manager/assessor views.
* Admin authoring studio with reusable blocks/templates, preview by device/profile, publishing workflow and rollback.

## Integrity, security and privacy

* Training drafts are Collaborative/Versioned; published regulated curricula are Controlled; completion/certification evidence is Sealed or Operational according to profile.
* Resource-level permissions for authors, reviewers, instructors, assessors, learners and auditors. Explain-access and simulation apply.
* Anti-cheating/proctoring features are proportionate, transparent and optional; do not default to invasive surveillance.
* Signed certificates/badges with verifiable identifiers, revocation and expiry. Completion evidence remains exportable.
* Full audit, retention, legal hold, erasure/tombstone and residency policies.

## Interoperability

* Eventiva-owned canonical contracts with replaceable storage/media providers.
* Evaluate and support suitable open learning standards such as xAPI/cmi5, SCORM import/export and verifiable credentials where they preserve offline, portability and security requirements; do not make a proprietary LMS provider mandatory.
* Import/export courses, question banks, learner records and evidence in documented open formats with fidelity reports.

## Agents and automation

* Every canonical learner/author/assessor action has UI/API/MCP/ACP parity and normal permission/purpose logging.
* Agents may recommend pathways, explain material, generate practice, identify gaps and chase expiry. They cannot self-award competency, alter sealed evidence or approve regulated content without explicit human authority.

Credential model:
- Completion, assessment, practical competency, qualification, licence, induction and certification are versioned signed credential/claim records on the worker profile with issuer, subject, scope, evidence, source content/version/profile, issue/valid-from/expiry, renewal, status and revocation.
- A credential is not itself an unrestricted role. Tenant policy explicitly maps an accepted credential type/issuer/level to one or more permission groups, role memberships, feature flags/surfaces or eligibility conditions.
- Roles/permission groups remain live relationship subjects. Prefer conditional/dynamic membership based on valid credentials rather than copying permanent grants.

Grant policy:
- Tenant administrators define whether an outcome auto-activates access, requires assessor approval, requires manager/security approval or only contributes to an eligibility decision.

Operational checks:
- Eligibility also considers qualification validity, site/event induction, role/resource permission, required supervision, suspension/restrictions, assurance level and refresher/recency requirements where lawful.
