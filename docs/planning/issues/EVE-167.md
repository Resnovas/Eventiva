## Goal

Each department keeps its notes, paperwork, safety documents and artist riders on the event, edited together by people and the agent, readable offline on site.

## Scope

1. Tables `production_document` (event_id, department_id, kind: note, rider, paperwork, safety, custom; locked_version; shared_with) and `rider` (performer contact_ref, document_id, status: received, reviewed, agreed).
2. Actions: `document.create`, `document.update` (Yjs update), `document.share` (confirm when shared outside the org), `document.lock` (freezes a version; later edits start a new version), `rider.submit`, `rider.review`.
3. External sharing: a vendor or artist granted a document sees only that document; revoking removes it from their device on next sync ([EVE-51](<https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-51/p112-build-collaboration-yjs-for-text-and-canvas-with-record-sync-and>)).
4. Agent: can draft and summarise documents, and extract rider requirements (input list, stage plot items, hospitality) into a suggested list for F1.9 and F1.11, applied only on confirmation.
   4a. Register a F4 `PartyReferenceOwner` (<issue id="7692d04c-3444-4989-b2aa-b73a1a69ab02" label="EVE-99" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-99/f44-find-and-merge-duplicate-contacts-and-import-and-export-them" />) for `Rider.performer contact_ref` so party merges rewrite it.
5. Screen: Documents (list per department, editor, sharing panel, version history) with projection and bound controls.

## Acceptance criteria

* Two people and the agent edit one document at once, one person offline, and it converges after reconnect with presence shown online.
* A locked version is immutable; an edit after lock creates version 2 and both are readable.
* An artist submits a rider PDF from a shared link and only sees their own riders.
* Revoking a vendor's share removes the document from their device on the next sync.
* Screen passes the unbound-control check and axe.
