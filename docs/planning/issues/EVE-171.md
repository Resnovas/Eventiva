## Linear description

## Goal

Every person on an event gets one schedule of where to be and when (calls, travel, hotel, meals, their segments and shifts), generated from the event and kept up to date, readable offline and in their own calendar.

## Context

* Parent: <issue id="fbcff205-decd-464a-b8ab-a1278e71fbe4" label="EVE-73" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-73/f1-events-and-production-planning-the-event-record-run-of-show" /> sections 4, 5 (Itinerary, ItineraryItem), 6 (Itinerary screen), 11 (personal data).
* Sources: F1.1 phases and team, F1.3 segments the person owns, F2 shifts (ItineraryItem kind `shift`, read through F2's published actions when F2 exists), F7 travel. Until F2 and F7 exist, those items are entered by hand.
* Scheduling primitives come from <issue id="97915f03-fb56-4efc-bb5a-15e8b62b96b2" label="EVE-33" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-33/port-scheduling-primitives-for-availability-bookings-and-rosters" />.

## Scope

1. Tables `itinerary` (event_id, audience: team member or group of members, published_version) and `itinerary_item` (kind: call, travel, hotel, meal, segment, shift, custom; starts_at, ends_at, location text or Space, source ref, notes).
2. Actions: `itinerary.generate` (builds or refreshes items from sources without overwriting hand-edited items), `itinerary.update`, `itinerary.publish` (confirm; versioned; notifies through <issue id="9aae6a50-4a43-4327-ae6a-51d18bc69b35" label="EVE-24" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-24/port-notifications-messaging-channels-and-transactional-email" />).
3. Calendar: a per-itinerary ICS feed link with a revocable token.
4. Personal data: dietary and access needs are opt-in fields with purpose text, visible only to production manager and above; deleted with the event retention period from the org configuration schema.
5. Agent: generates itineraries for a group, flags overlapping items for the same person, and answers "where should Sam be at 14:00 on load-in day".
6. Screen: Itinerary (agenda by day, filter per person or group, publish) with projection and bound controls; crew see only their own.

## Acceptance criteria

* Generating twice is idempotent; a hand-edited item survives regeneration.
* A crew member sees only their itinerary, offline, after one sync.
* The ICS feed opens in a standard calendar client and stops working after the token is revoked.
* Overlap check flags two items for the same person; the agent's suggestion only applies on confirmation.
* Health-related fields are hidden from crew and vendors in a permission test.
* Screen passes the unbound-control check and axe.
