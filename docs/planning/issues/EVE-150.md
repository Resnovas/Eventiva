## Scope

1. Table `inventory_discrepancy`: type (`missing`, `extra`, `damaged`, `wrong-item`, `double-checkout`, `negative-stock`), refs (asset, product, location, movements, pick list, event), notes doc, photos, assignee, status, resolution (`found`, `lost`, `repaired`, `written-off`, `adjusted`, `duplicate-scan`).
2. Actions `inventory.discrepancy.raise`, `assign`, `resolve`. Resolving as `lost` or `written-off` needs confirm and writes a movement to the virtual lost or scrap location; `adjusted` writes an adjustment movement; `duplicate-scan` writes nothing.
3. Automatic raising from `inventory:negative_stock`, double checkout and pick variances, deduplicated per cause.
4. Actions `inventory.asset.quarantine` and `release` (release needs confirm, is online only, and is refused while a failed safety inspection stands, F6.7). Damaged reports quarantine the asset by default.
5. Screen Discrepancies (inbox and detail) with <issue id="91297309-acc4-4bb9-ad68-33fbc2720e5a" label="EVE-50" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-50/p111-build-screen-awareness-app-state-navigation-and-the-screen" /> projection and bound controls.

## Acceptance criteria

* A negative level after an offline merge creates exactly one `negative-stock` discrepancy, even when merged twice.
* A damage report with a photo quarantines the asset; the asset leaves availability immediately.
* Quarantine on device A offline and release on device B offline leave the asset quarantined after sync.
* Resolving as `lost` moves the asset to the lost location and emits a write-off for F11.
* The agent can raise and propose a resolution but `lost` and `written-off` stop for confirm.
