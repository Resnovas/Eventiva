## 1. Goal

An organiser sells products, services and tickets for an event online and in person, from one catalogue and one order list, takes payment even when the venue loses connectivity, and fulfils, refunds and reconciles every sale without leaving Eventiva.

## 2. Users and jobs

| User | Jobs |
| -- | -- |
| Organiser or sales manager | Build the catalogue, set prices per event and channel, open sales channels (online store, booths, box office), set offline limits, watch sales live, approve refunds |
| Cashier (crew on a phone, tablet or desktop) | Open a register session with a cash float, ring up sales fast, take card or cash, print or send a receipt, keep selling offline, close the session and count cash |
| Fulfilment staff | Pick and pack shipped orders, hand over click-and-collect and on-site collection orders, mark digital items delivered |
| Finance | See payouts, fees, refunds and cash variances; export to accounting (F11) |
| Attendee or customer | Browse the store, pay as a guest or signed in, see order history, get receipts, request a refund |
| Vendor (F5 directory listing) | Sell its own products at an organiser's event under the organiser's rules, receive its share (slice F9.12) |
| Agent | Everything a person with the same permissions can do: draft catalogues from a supplier list, answer "what sold at booth 3", prepare refunds for approval, flag reconciliation gaps |

## 3. Market check

Checked 2026-11-01 against vendor documentation. Names stay out of customer-facing copy.

* **Shopify (online store plus POS).** One catalogue, customer list and order list across online and in-person. Offline card payments on supported readers only, with a per-device daily limit and a per-order limit set by the merchant; payments are captured on reconnect, reconnecting within 24 hours is recommended, and declines are found only after reconnect. Tap to Pay and manual card entry do not work offline. ([help](<https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/selling-offline/offline-payments>))
* **Square POS.** Offline payments must be uploaded within 72 hours (24 recommended) or they expire unrecoverably; the seller sets a per-transaction maximum and bears expired, declined and disputed offline payments. Tap to Pay, gift cards and keyed cards are not supported offline. ([help](<https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/7777>))
* **Stripe Terminal (payment platform, the likely first adapter).** Store-and-forward offline payments on Bluetooth readers and smart readers through the iOS, Android and React Native SDKs; Tap to Pay on Android has no offline mode, Tap to Pay on iPhone offline is in private preview; in the EEA offline payments need chip and PIN. ([docs](<https://docs.stripe.com/terminal/features/operate-offline/overview>))

Where Eventiva matches: one catalogue and order list for every channel; offline card sales with explicit per-device and per-order limits and a visible risk; cash always works offline.

Where Eventiva differs: channels are scoped to an F1 Event and Phase (a booth, a box office, the online store for that event) rather than to a shop; tickets (F8), stock (F6) and bookable services (F2) plug into one checkout through a line provider contract; cashiers use the same app as every other role, unlocked by permission; every operation is an action an agent can call (<issue id="5c7aeec2-d49c-4028-9eca-1bc641d3618c" label="EVE-13" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-13/establish-modular-core-foundations-for-database-adapters-expo-and" /> parity rule), including refunds under approval.

## 7. Agent behaviour

* When asked: create catalogues from a pasted list or file, set prices, open a channel, answer sales questions from sales.report.summary, prepare refunds, chase failed offline payments by drafting customer messages.
* Proactively (behind the flag, as suggestions in the agent thread, never silent changes): warn when an open channel has products without stock, a register is near its offline limit, an offline batch has not forwarded within 12 hours, a register session closed with a cash variance, or reconciliation finds a mismatch.
* Approval: refunds and payment voids always need a person (`always` for agents); price changes on an open channel, channel opening, provider connection and split rules need confirmation. The agent can never move money out (payouts are provider-driven) and never sees card data or provider secrets.
* Offline: the agent on a register can read the local catalogue and queue an offline sale like a cashier; it says plainly that payment confirmation waits for connectivity.

## 11. Compliance

* **Card data (PCI DSS):** Eventiva never sees or stores card numbers. Online uses the provider's hosted fields (aim SAQ A); card-present uses provider-certified readers. No card data in logs, telemetry, actions or agent context.
* **Strong customer authentication (UK and EEA):** online card payments support 3DS through the provider; offline EEA card payments need chip and PIN.
* **Consumer law:** UK and EU distance selling gives a 14-day cancellation right for goods; dated leisure services and tickets are exempt. Per-product refund policy fields and checkout text must say which applies.
* **Tax:** prices for consumers shown tax inclusive where required; receipts show tax rate and amount; VAT receipts on request. Records kept 6 years (UK) or the tenant's configured period.
* **Age-restricted products** (alcohol at events): product flag that forces an age check prompt on the register and blocks online sale unless enabled.
* **Personal data (UK GDPR):** guest checkout collects only what fulfilment needs; marketing consent separate and unticked; customer data lives in F4 contacts.
* **Merchant of record:** each tenant is the merchant of record through a connected account (D2), so chargebacks, offline declines and tax belong to the tenant, stated in onboarding.

## 12. Acceptance

* An organiser creates a product, opens an online channel and a register channel for one F1 Event, and sells the same product in both; both orders appear in one list with correct tax.
* A register sells offline (cash and card on a supported reader) through a simulated outage, the device restarts, and on reconnect every sale uploads once, payments forward, stock deltas reach F6 and one simulated decline surfaces as a task for the organiser.
* Per-device and per-order offline limits block a sale that would exceed them.
* A ticket line (F8) and a stock line (F6) in one cart reserve, commit on payment and release on cancel through the line provider contract.
* A refund requested by the agent waits for a person's approval and then refunds through the provider and restocks.
* Every action in section 4 is in the inventory and passes the parity check; every screen in section 6 has a projection and no unbound control.
* Tenant isolation: a cashier in org A cannot read org B's orders by any surface (UI, HTTP, MCP).
* Webhooks are signature checked and idempotent; replaying one changes nothing.
