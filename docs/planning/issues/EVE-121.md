## Linear description

## Goal

HR can write contract and policy templates together, generate a document for a worker with their details merged in, send it for signature and get back a signed copy with evidence that holds up, without an external e-signature vendor in phase one.

## Scope

1. Tables DocumentTemplate (versioned, body as Yjs document), HrDocument, SignatureRequest, SignatureEvent, Policy, Acknowledgement.
2. Actions `hr.documentTemplates.create` / `.update`, `hr.documents.generate`, `hr.documents.upload`, `hr.signatures.request`, `.sign`, `.decline`, `.void`, `hr.policies.publish`, `hr.policies.acknowledge`.
3. Merge fields drawn from Worker, Engagement, Position and the tenant (F4 Organisation), validated by Schema; a template with an unknown field fails to publish.
4. Rendering a generated document to PDF as the signing copy; the document hash is stored before signing.
5. `ESignature` port with a built-in adapter: signer identity from the authenticated session (<issue id="7f612e80-3332-4acf-a74c-d35db06fe4e8" label="EVE-43" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-43/p14-build-identity-and-access-orgs-authorization-user-profiles-and" />), explicit consent text, typed or drawn signature, timestamp, device and network details, document hash before and after, and a sealed evidence page appended to the PDF.
6. `hr.signatures.sign` and `hr.policies.acknowledge` are human-only: the agent can prepare and send, never sign or acknowledge (action metadata enforces it on every surface).
7. Signing needs connectivity; offline the screen says so and queues nothing.
8. Starter templates (whitelabel, ASCII): written statement of particulars, freelancer agreement, volunteer agreement, 48-hour opt-out, confidentiality agreement.

## Acceptance criteria

* Two HR users edit a template at once and converge (<issue id="a1348819-43cc-4005-8e8d-a896a761af03" label="EVE-51" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-51/p112-build-collaboration-yjs-for-text-and-canvas-with-record-sync-and" />); publishing creates a new version and documents keep the version they were generated from.
* A generated contract signed by the worker and the company signatory produces a PDF whose evidence page matches the stored hashes; altering one byte fails verification.
* The agent, asked to sign on someone's behalf, is refused on UI, MCP and ACP with a typed `HumanOnlyAction` error.
* Unsigned documents past due date appear in `hr.reports.compliance` and produce a reminder.
