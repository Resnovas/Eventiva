## Goal

An organiser issues a numbered, frozen revision of the plan, sends it to safety advisers and authorities through a secure expiring link, and collects their comments and approval on the record.

## Scope

1. Tables `sitemap_revision`, `sitemap_approval`, `sitemap_share_link`.
2. `sitemap.revision.issue` (approval: always): snapshot of all layer documents (Yjs state vectors and encoded state) and mirror rows, numbered per map, with purpose (for comment, for approval, for construction, for event) and notes; generates the default PDF export (F10.6).
3. `sitemap.revision.approve` and `.reject` by account users with the permission or by share-link reviewers allowed to approve; append-only.
4. `sitemap.share.create` (approval: always) and `.revoke`: token stored as a hash, expiry required (default 30 days, maximum from settings), optional password, chosen layers, can comment, can approve. Share viewers get a read-only map, never the live editable documents.
5. Compare two revisions: features added, moved (with distance), removed, per layer.
6. Notifications to the issuer on comment, approval and rejection.

## Acceptance criteria

* An issued revision does not change when the live map is edited afterwards.
* A share link shows only its layers, stops working at expiry and immediately on revoke, and the password is checked server-side with rate limiting.
* An approval through a share link records the reviewer's entered name and email, the link id and time in the audit log.
* Compare lists a moved stage with the distance moved.

## Constraints

Effect-TS v3; no new dependency without approval here; no viewer identity in PostHog events for share links; ships to main with check results.
