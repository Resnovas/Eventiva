## Linear description

## Goal

The plan produces the shopping list (counts, metres, areas per symbol) for hire, picking and costing, and prints or exports at a true scale that crew and authorities can rely on.

## Context

* Parent <issue id="f8432c08-24bb-4690-a3e1-16ed79977eee" label="EVE-108" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-108/f10-interactive-site-maps" /> sections 4 and 12. Quantities feed F6 (inventory reservations) and, through F6, F11 costs; F10 never writes F6 tables directly.
* Market check: OnePlan exports a bill of quantities to CSV or Excel and prints to A0; this is the parity bar.

## Scope

1. `sitemap.quantities.compute`: rows of symbol, category, count, total length, total area, linked F6 inventory item type, per layer and in total; result stored as a quantities snapshot.
2. `sitemap.quantities.sendToInventory` (approval: confirm): calls the F6 reservation action registered through the link registry, with the snapshot id for traceability.
3. `sitemap.export` as a durable job (<issue id="a626308a-598f-41ba-accd-4004cbbd9932" label="EVE-54" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-54/p18c-build-durable-agent-runs-on-effect-cluster-and-workflow" />): PDF and PNG at A4 to A0, portrait or landscape, at a chosen scale (1:200 to 1:5000) or fit-to-page, with title block (site, event, phase, revision, date, issuer), scale bar, north arrow, legend, grid reference, data attribution and the capacity note; CSV of quantities; GeoJSON of chosen layers in WGS84.
4. `PrintRenderer` port: server-side render of the same style spec the client uses, so prints match the screen. MapLibre renders raster, so A0 at 300 dpi (about 14,000 by 9,900 px, above common GPU texture limits) is rendered in tiles and stitched into the PDF; title block, scale bar and legend are vector PDF elements.
5. Exported files stored through <issue id="8d8d301b-d679-4dac-95bb-c24889179cd0" label="EVE-25" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-25/port-files-private-attachments-artifacts-and-document-import" /> and attached to the map or revision.

## Acceptance criteria

* Quantities for a fixture map match a hand count, including fence panels from line lengths.
* A0 PDF at 1:1000: a 100 m reference line measures 100 mm on the printed page (checked in the PDF geometry) and attribution is present.
* GeoJSON round-trips into a standard GIS viewer within 0.1 m of the source positions.
* Sending quantities to F6 creates exactly one reservation request per snapshot (idempotent on retry).

## Constraints

Effect-TS v3; renderer library needs approval here before use; no new dependency without approval; ships to main with check results.
