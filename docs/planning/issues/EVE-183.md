## Linear description

## Goal

The map tells organisers and the agent how big things are, how many people fit, whether routes are wide enough and clear, and which rules the layout breaks, before anyone builds it.

## Context

* F1 `Space.site_map_zone_ref` points at Zones defined here (<issue id="fbcff205-decd-464a-b8ab-a1278e71fbe4" label="EVE-73" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-73/f1-events-and-production-planning-the-event-record-run-of-show" />); F8 access zones may reference them too.

## Scope

1. `sitemap.measure`: length, area, perimeter, bearing in site-local metres (pure functions in shared contracts).
2. `sitemap.zone.define`: polygon feature becomes a Zone with usable area (area minus contained obstacle footprints), capacity mode (computed from density per m2, or fixed), access rule refs. Recomputed by the mirror on every change. This is holding capacity only: the zone also shows exit capacity (sum of clear widths of the routes leaving it, times the rule set's flow rate per metre per minute, times the evacuation time) and reports the lower of the two, as the Green Guide's safe capacity is the lowest of holding, entry, exit and emergency exit capacity.
3. `sitemap.route.define`: line feature becomes a Route (emergency, vehicle, pedestrian, accessible) with minimum width; the measured clear width is the narrowest gap between obstacles along the route.
4. Rule sets in settings (<issue id="9d08c369-bac9-4eb7-b8a7-329b5ec9bff0" label="EVE-41" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-41/p12-build-app-config-settings-secrets-and-credentials" />), Effect Schema validated: facility ratios (for example toilets per 100 attendees by duration), minimum route widths, density limits, clearance distances (for example generator to tent), boundary containment. Starter rule sets cite their source documents in their description, editable per tenant, and hold only openly published figures: Purple Guide public ratios (one female toilet per 100, one male per 500 plus one urinal per 150, at least one accessible toilet; the full guide is subscription-only), Green Guide capacity method with tenant-entered flow rates and P and S factors, IBC Table 1004.5 or NFPA 101 occupant load factors (7 net sq ft per person chairs only, 5 standing).
5. `sitemap.check.run` returns findings with severity, rule id, feature ids and a human sentence; runs automatically (debounced) after edits through the change feed.
6. Attendance input comes from F1 Event attendance or F8 sales, read through registered contracts.

## Acceptance criteria

* A fixture map with seeded faults (overlap, item outside boundary, 2.1 m emergency route where 3.0 m is required, zone over capacity, toilets short by ratio, generator too close to a tent) returns exactly those findings.
* Capacity of a 1,000 m2 zone at 2 persons per m2 with 100 m2 of obstacles is 1,800; with one 2 m exit route at a fixture flow rate of 60 persons per metre per minute and 8 minutes it is 960, and the zone reports 960 with exit capacity as the limit.
* Every exported capacity figure carries the "calculated estimate" note.

## Constraints

Effect-TS v3; geometry pure TypeScript; no new dependency without approval here (a geometry library such as a polygon clipper needs approval on this issue); ships to main with check results.
