## BINDING owner decision — 15 November 2026: human-in-the-loop for ALL operational emergency actions

**Never automatically actuate a show-control or operational response from an incident, alarm, emergency sensor, issue classification, agent interpretation, integration alert or rules-engine event.** An alarm/event may be ingested, classified, displayed, logged, correlated, escalated to people, propose a configured SOP or preset, and notify designated staff; it must NOT automatically GO/hold/pause/mute audio, set venue lights/DMX, trigger PA/media/announcements, start emergency scene/preset, dispatch units or carry out other consequential incident-response operations. The possibility of false alarms and resulting public panic/harm is the rationale. This is an unconditional product policy for **operational incidents and alarms**, not an admin-configurable bypass or an exception for 'tested integration'. A permissioned, accountable **human must deliberately initiate** the approved action following assessment; log initiator, confirmation, linked incident, preset/version, result, and failures. Provide quick incident-linked actions but not default-on automatic acceptance.

**Differentiate ordinary show automation:** separately programmed and human-armed cue stacks, playback sequences, lighting console input, MIDI/OSC/MSC and timecode may continue to start permitted *non-emergency show cues* according to show programming; that is not authority for automatically activating an incident/emergency response. Incoming external signals claiming to be alarms are treated as alerts for human evaluation even if received over a show-control protocol. An external operator deliberately firing an authorized ordinary cue is still allowed, subject to arming and permissions.

**System-of-systems boundary:** independent certified fire alarms, evacuation/voice alarm, emergency lighting and physical overrides retain their own mandatory automatic functions. Eventiva's human-in-the-loop restriction is for Eventiva-initiated control actions and must not inhibit certified systems, lawful fire-panel interlocks, or physical emergency interventions. Do not call for an extra Eventiva confirmation where it could delay or prevent those independent measures. Any proposed direct control of safety-certified hardware requires separate safety engineering and approval.

**Acceptance criteria:** simulate a false fire/heat alarm and a mistakenly classified crowd incident: both notify MDT with a proposed response and initiate **zero** show/lighting/audio/PA/dispatch outputs without a deliberate authorized human action; no auto approval through agents, tenant rules or remote console input marked as emergency. Authorized manual response from MDT executes only the selected versioned preset and clearly reports partial success/failure. Ordinary human-armed playback and mapped external non-emergency cue input still work independently. Test no deferred/replayed emergency output after reconnect.

Implementation and dependencies remain unapproved.

## Decision status and purpose

**Approved product behavior — 2026-11-15.** Owner approved the design choice that guided Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) are **advisory by default**, with a tenant administrator able to require particular procedures/steps, supporting evidence, and authorized sign-off. This issue defines a **shared guided-procedure capability**, not a parallel MDT-only workflow engine. This is specification approval only; implementation, new dependency versions, integrations and production changes require normal authorization.

The live operational system receives actual pre-existing **issues, requests, incidents, tasks and alerts**. Guided procedures attach to and advance those records; **there is no simulated 999/101 call-intake product** in the live F7 control-room scope. Lightweight scenario simulation remains an optional <issue id="9268485b-c9f1-49ed-93b7-a3c90b9bc7c6" label="EVE-245" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-245/build-the-integrated-training-competency-and-certification-platform" /> **training submodule** using strictly isolated synthetic state.

## Goals

A controller, event technician, dispatcher, crew leader, support agent or learner can follow an appropriate, versioned procedure against an existing Eventiva record. Examples: assessing and escalating an incident, handling a production/audio fault, checking an equipment or vehicle defect, triaging an IT service issue, and preparing an operational closeout.

One canonical Effect Schema action/Effect Workflow model serves web, mobile/MDT, API, agent and protocol adapters. Screen-awareness projections show the current procedure, required actions, approvals and outstanding steps. Procedure publication uses Eventiva's controlled content/change governance, not AI-authored live instructions.

## Scope and policy

1. **Definitions:** tenant- and module-owned `ProcedureDefinition` with immutable published version, author, competent approver for regulated/controlled steps, purpose, applicability (record type, severity, service/department, event, capability and terminology profile), locale, prerequisites, response deadlines/expiry, content provenance, and policy override rules. Draft/edit/review/publish/supersede/retire with audit and historical version fidelity.
2. **Execution:** `ProcedureRun` linked to the **original** issue/task/incident (not an automatic duplicate CAD), assigned operator(s), owner/team, record state and current procedure version; tracked step attempts, device/server timestamps, input, decisions, evidence, escalations, skip/override reasons and durable receipts. Support pause/resume, reassignment/handover, restart/recovery and immutable history/corrections.
3. **Steps:** informational guidance, question/input, conditional branch, document or data lookup, validated Eventiva action, evidence/attachment, check, escalation, named approval/sign-off and closeout condition. Steps may be optional or marked required; *default new procedure/step is advisory*. Tenant policy can make chosen procedures or steps mandatory by context, including evidence and approver/competency. Mark critical safeguards as non-skippable at the applicable controlled policy floor; ordinary advisory skips are logged with reason when requested. **No tenant override weakens platform-enforced authorization or an applicable regulated/safety rule.**
4. **Actions and rules:** bind executable steps to pre-existing canonical Eventiva actions (<issue id="dc89a6af-338c-417b-a352-80cbfb05ec1e" label="EVE-40" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-40/p11-build-the-action-contract-and-inventory-in-effect-schema" />/<issue id="7f612e80-3332-4acf-a74c-d35db06fe4e8" label="EVE-43" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-43/p14-build-identity-and-access-orgs-authorization-user-profiles-and" />) with fresh permissions, current state/version, idempotency, human approvals and append-only audit; use <issue id="55c2d64a-d779-41d9-b74e-dcc7d062b86b" label="EVE-252" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-252/implement-effect-cluster-sharding-and-multi-runner-recovery-in-the" /> Effect Cluster/Workflow for durable execution and F3/F14's single rule engine for triggers/notifications. Rules may suggest/start procedures when authorized; they cannot bypass step approval or silently self-sign protected actions. No second special-purpose automation system.
5. **Live control integration:** on actual incoming F7/F1/F3 incidents/requests, offer matching guidance; maintain incident priority/assessment and reason trail, owner/control-group handover, comms template and acknowledgments, dispatcher/crew progress, required closeout forms and policy-driven closure gates. Avoid hardcoded police/fire/ambulance standards; configured roleplay and operational profiles are mapped to stable semantics (<issue id="338aae77-d10e-4f8a-9189-fe3655bd3572" label="EVE-28" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-28/port-localization-for-people-and-for-the-agent" />).
6. **Offline & safety:** allow viewing cached approved procedures, collecting routine answers and queueing allowed evidence/notes offline. Authoritative actions—dispatch, allocation, safety-critical state, live control, irreversible approvals—stay online and revalidate; show stale procedure/version warnings and resolve conflicts on reconnect. A deployment without a separate safety case is for coordination, not certified emergency-service operation.
7. **Agent-native:** identical discoverability and action semantics for human and delegated agents; permission-filtered suggested SOPs, explanations, version/evidence gaps and next steps. Agents may recommend and draft but not invent safety procedures, falsely claim verification, approve their own actions or issue practical competency.
8. **Training reuse:** <issue id="9268485b-c9f1-49ed-93b7-a3c90b9bc7c6" label="EVE-245" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-245/build-the-integrated-training-competency-and-certification-platform" /> optional lightweight branching scenarios reuse published SOP content/terminology and view patterns, but run with **synthetic, separately scoped records and test-only action projections** that can NEVER send real dispatch/messages, write live operations, alter external services or issue credentials. Record learning outcomes and exact content/profile version; instructor sign-off remains separate.

## Minimum acceptance tests

* An existing technical issue launches a branched advisory SOP; an optional step can be skipped with recorded reason, while the linked issue remains the source of truth.
* Tenant admin publishes a controlled procedure that requires specified evidence and an independent authorized sign-off; closure without them fails on **UI, API and agent** paths.
* Offline responses to a routine checklist reconcile in order with visible conflict and version changes; no dispatch, sign-off or restricted action executes offline.
* Mid-run operator handover preserves in-progress answers, ownership, timestamps and immutable audit; runner restart replays safely without duplicated applied steps.
* A revoked user/agent lacks permissions on resume; attempts to self-approve, to bypass a mandatory step or to fabricate confirmation fail closed.
* A training scenario runs on synthetic fixtures; test proves no writes/messages/dispatch reach live Eventiva operational services.
* Procedure/terminology revisions do not rewrite historical evidence or accidentally replace the original issue/CAD.
