## Goal

Organisers run promotions (percentage, fixed, buy x get y, bundles, codes, automatic discounts) and sell or issue gift cards that work online and in person, with limits enforced even when registers were offline.

## Context

* Parent <issue id="7d939472-20ba-4512-b231-c0f04c05574c" label="EVE-107" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-107/f9-online-and-in-person-sales-storefront-point-of-sale-orders-payments" /> sections 4 and 5. F8 access codes that unlock hidden ticket types stay in F8; this slice covers price reductions on any line kind, including tickets.

## Scope

1. Discount rules: percent, fixed amount, buy x get y, bundle price, free item; scope by product, variant, line kind, channel, F1 Event; start and end; per-customer and total usage limits; stackable flag and priority.
2. Discount codes (single or bulk generated) and automatic discounts.
3. Deterministic cart evaluation in F9.2 totals with an explanation per line (shown to people and the agent).
4. Offline: rules cached; usage counted as deltas; usage limits enforced on the server; over-use after offline sync flagged for review, never reverses the sale.
5. Gift cards: sell as a product kind or issue manually; code stored as a hash; balance ledger (issue, redeem, refund back, adjust); redeem online and at the register (online only, as in the market check); expiry per local law setting.
6. Actions: sales.discount.create, update, archive; sales.cart.applyDiscount; sales.giftCard.issue, adjust (confirm), redeem.
7. Discounts and gift cards screen.

## Acceptance criteria

* Table-driven tests for every rule kind and stacking order, including rounding with allocation from F9.1.
* A code with a limit of 10 used 12 times across two offline registers yields 12 sales and one review flag.
* Gift card partially redeemed online and then at a register shows the right balance; a refund returns value to the card.
