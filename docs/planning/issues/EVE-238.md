## Linear description

## Goal

Site maps work just as well for an arena, a conference centre, a theatre, a custom-built structure or a festival build as for an outdoor field. Organisers draw or import multi-level buildings with walls, rooms, doors, stairs, seating bowls and services, with or without a street map underneath.

## Scope

1. Site kinds (field on Site): `outdoor` (geo-referenced, basemap on by default), `venue` (building or campus, levels, basemap optional), `plan` (no geography at all: blank canvas in metres, for custom structures, festival builds on an unsurveyed field, or a design before the site is chosen). A `plan` site can be anchored to a real location later without moving any feature.
2. Building elements as feature kinds with metric attributes: wall (thickness, height), room or area (name, use, area, occupancy limit), door or opening (clear width, fire exit flag, direction), column, stair, ramp, lift and escalator (as vertical connectors linking two or more levels), fixed seating section and arena bowl (rows and tiers as geometry only), stage, rigging point and grid (position and reference to an F1 DesignElement; no load calculation), service point (power, water, data, gas), loading dock and vehicle access.
3. Drawing tools in the editor (extends <issue id="c2c22f6e-0703-449c-a01d-0a6552d1d9fc" label="EVE-178" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-178/f103-map-editor-screen-with-screen-awareness" />): wall tool with snapping and auto-joined corners, room from enclosed walls, door placed into a wall, orthogonal and angle snapping, dimensions and offset input by keyboard, copy a level to a new level.
4. Levels: level switcher with ghosted level below, vertical connectors drawn across levels, mezzanines and partial levels, level heights.
5. Nested plans: any footprint feature (a marquee, a building, a container stage) can open its own interior plan as a child map with its own levels, so a festival site holds detailed tent interiors and an arena holds its back-of-house.
6. Venue templates in the starter library: arena (bowl, floor, concourse, back of house), theatre (stage, fly tower footprint, stalls, circle), exhibition and conference hall (grid of stands), marquee interiors, festival arena enclosure.
7. Imports, as reference or as editable features: DXF layers mapped to walls, doors and rooms by a layer mapping step; IMDF (the open indoor mapping JSON format) imported to levels, units, openings and amenities; PDF floor plans as calibrated reference (<issue id="9b8adbfb-05a9-4f10-9c60-baa5bfcfd4fa" label="EVE-146" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-146/f101-sites-levels-base-layers-and-calibration" />). No new dependency without approval here.
8. Checks across levels (extends <issue id="0a770823-74c2-43b8-849a-45631c15d9e5" label="EVE-183" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-183/f105-measurements-zones-routes-and-rule-checks" />): exit width per room from its doors, travel routes through doors and vertical connectors to exits, occupancy per room against its limit, step-free route availability between levels.
9. Public and crew maps (<issue id="c67f094b-8c19-4427-8672-51c0648b44fd" label="EVE-202" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-202/f108-offline-map-packs-and-the-crew-map" />, <issue id="cadefb17-f505-4ddc-9238-ad3d83926bf3" label="EVE-207" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-207/f1010-public-attendee-map-and-website-embed" />) show levels and indoor routes: "find the nearest accessible toilet on this level".

## Acceptance criteria

* A `plan` site with no basemap renders, edits and measures correctly on web and Android, and can be anchored to a real location later with all features kept.
* A three-level arena fixture (bowl, concourse, back of house) is drawn with walls, rooms, doors, stairs and lifts; the agent answers "how many exits does level 2 have and what is their total width" from the actions.
* An IMDF sample venue and a DXF floor plan import to editable levels.
* A marquee on a festival site opens its interior plan, and its interior features appear in the site quantities.
* Cross-level checks flag a room whose exit width is below the rule set, and a level with no step-free route.

## Constraints

Effect-TS v3; geometry pure TypeScript in shared contracts; WCAG 2.2 AA for every new tool, including keyboard drawing; no new dependency without approval here; ships to main with check results; flag `f10-site-maps`.
