## Linear description

## Goal

Attendees find stages, toilets, food, first aid, accessible routes and exits on a public map in the app and on the event website, offline once loaded, accessible to everyone.

## Context

* Parent <issue id="f8432c08-24bb-4690-a3e1-16ed79977eee" label="EVE-108" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-108/f10-interactive-site-maps" /> sections 4 (PublicMap), 6 (Public map) and 11 (accessibility, licences). Builds on revisions (F10.7). F14 website builder embeds it as a block; F14 owns the page, F10 owns the map block contract.

## Scope

1. `sitemap.public.publish` (approval: always) and `.unpublish`: from an issued revision, chosen public layers and points of interest (name, category, description, opening times, accessibility notes).
2. Public read endpoint (no account) serving the published revision, cached at the edge and in the app; attendee location stays on the device.
3. Public map screen in the app and a web embed block with a stable contract (`sitemap.publicMap.v1`: map id, initial viewport, categories) for F14.
4. POI list and search as the text alternative; filters by category; accessible route layer highlighted on request.
5. Offline: the app caches the published map and its tiles after first open.
6. Whitelabel: no vendor names in attendee UI; attribution shown as the licences require.

## Acceptance criteria

* Unpublishing removes the public map from the endpoint at once and from the app cache on next open.
* axe reports no serious issues; every POI is reachable by keyboard and screen reader through the list.
* The embed renders in an F14 test page and on the app's event screen from the same contract.
* No draft or non-public layer is reachable through the public endpoint (negative test).

## Constraints

WCAG 2.2 AA; whitelabel copy, ASCII only; Effect-TS v3; flag `f10-public-map`; no new dependency without approval here; ships to main with check results.
