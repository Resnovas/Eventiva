## Linear description

## Goal

Warehouse and event crews prep, load and return kit by scanning, on a phone or a hardware scanner, with or without signal, and the record is correct afterwards.

## Context

* Parent <issue id="928dc098-5cc9-4586-88bb-50abbe03071a" label="EVE-63" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-63/f6-warehousing-and-inventory-equipment-stock-availability-purchasing" /> decisions D3 (camera barcode and QR plus hardware scanners in keyboard mode; RFID later) and D4 (offline scan of an item already out is accepted and becomes a `double-checkout` discrepancy on sync).
* Scans are movements (F6.2), so two devices scanning the same pick list merge without conflict; line counts are derived.
* ScannerView is a new Rambo-UI component; the Expo stack comes from <issue id="a7a66a5c-e747-4245-bc84-3232ef65a16c" label="EVE-16" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-16/s3-prove-runtime-loading-and-switching-of-a-remote-module-on-expo-web" />. Camera barcode reading on Expo needs a new dependency (for example the Expo camera module); that is listed here for Jonathan's approval and is not to be added before it.
* Pick list assignees are F2 rostered people.

## Scope

1. Tables `inventory_pick_list` (reservation, assignees, status `open`, `in-progress`, `complete`) and `inventory_pick_line` (reservation line, expected qty or asset; scanned counts derived from movements).
2. Actions `inventory.prep.createPickList` (from a confirmed reservation; kits expand to components; containers pack lists), `assign`, `scanOut` (asset tag code or product plus qty; writes a movement from the warehouse to the event-site or vehicle location), `scanIn` (reverse), `complete` (confirm when variances exist; emits `inventory:pick_completed` with variance counts).
3. Scan rules: scanning a container scans its contents; scanning an asset not on the list warns and records it as `extra`; scanning a quarantined asset or one with an overdue required inspection (F6.7) is refused with `AssetNotFitForUse` online and offline (the device holds the due dates).
4. Rambo-UI `ScannerView`: camera input behind a `Scanner` port, hardware scanner keyboard input with no dependency, manual entry fallback, haptic and sound confirmation, large targets for gloved use.
5. Screens Prep and pick, and Check-in, with <issue id="91297309-acc4-4bb9-ad68-33fbc2720e5a" label="EVE-50" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-50/p111-build-screen-awareness-app-state-navigation-and-the-screen" /> projections (pick list, expected vs scanned, last scan, focused line) and bound controls.
6. Telemetry: `inventory_scan_recorded` with offline flag; scan-to-confirm latency metric.

## Acceptance criteria

* A 40-line pick list scanned by two offline phones at once completes with correct counts after both reconnect.
* Scanning the same serialised asset out on two offline devices leaves two movements and one `double-checkout` discrepancy after sync.
* A quarantined asset is refused offline with `AssetNotFitForUse`.
* Median scan-to-confirm under 300 ms on a mid-range Android device, measured and pasted here.
* The agent, asked "check in everything from the festival that is on this truck", runs `scanIn` for the vehicle contents and reports what is still missing.
* Web and Android both pass the <issue id="91297309-acc4-4bb9-ad68-33fbc2720e5a" label="EVE-50" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-50/p111-build-screen-awareness-app-state-navigation-and-the-screen" /> unbound-control check.
