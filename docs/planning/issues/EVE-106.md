## Linear description

## Goal

A driver completes a vehicle walkaround check on their phone with no signal, reports defects with photos, and a serious defect takes the vehicle off road and flags its bookings.

## Context

* Parent: [EVE-71](<https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-71>) actions `fleet.inspection.*`, `fleet.defect.*`, `fleet.vehicle.setRoadStatus`, `fleet.odometer.record`; entities InspectionTemplate, Inspection, Defect; journey J2.
* Supports operators' daily check duties (for example DVSA walkaround guidance) without claiming certification (parent section 11).
* Offline writes use the sync engine chosen in [EVE-17](<https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-17>).

## Scope

1. InspectionTemplate per VehicleType, versioned: items, required photos, severity if failed (advisory, fix soon, off road). A default template ships for van, minibus and HGV, editable by the tenant.
2. Actions: `fleet.inspection.start`, `submit` (answers, photos, odometer, device time), `fleet.defect.raise`, `triage`, `close`, `fleet.vehicle.setRoadStatus`. Inspections and defects are append-only and created offline.
3. Defect state machine: open, triaged, in repair, closed; illegal transitions replayed from offline are rejected with a typed error and the author is told.
4. Off-road rule: a failed item with severity off road sets the vehicle off road on the server, and every future booking of that vehicle gets a warning for F7.3 and F7.12.
5. Mobile Inspection screen with Checklist, camera and odometer input; projection includes template, answers so far and offline state.
6. Returning a vehicle to on road asks confirm and records who did it.

## Acceptance criteria

* In airplane mode, a full inspection with two photos and one off-road defect is stored; on reconnect it syncs, the vehicle goes off road and its bookings show the warning.
* A defect closed on the server while an offline device adds a note: the note is kept as a new entry, the closed state stands.
* Odometer lower than the last reading is flagged, not stored as current.
* The agent can submit an inspection from a spoken or typed checklist only for the signed-in driver.
