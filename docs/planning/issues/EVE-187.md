## Linear description

## Goal

The company buys what it needs through approved requests and orders, receives it into stock by scanning, and finance can match every supplier bill to what arrived.

## Context

* Parent <issue id="928dc098-5cc9-4586-88bb-50abbe03071a" label="EVE-63" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-63/f6-warehousing-and-inventory-equipment-stock-availability-purchasing" /> decision D5 (recommended): F6 owns requests, orders, receiving and reorder points; F11 (<issue id="158ba369-976e-47ea-9f86-a8df608cb4d0" label="EVE-69" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-69/f11-accounting-ledgers-invoices-bills-event-budgets-and-tax" />) owns supplier bills and postings. Agreed with the F11 thread 2026-11-01.
* Suppliers are F4 organisations; referenced by the F4 Contact id. Tax codes are F11's (`accounting.tax.code.*`); F6 stores F11 tax code ids, never its own rates. Amounts are integer minor units with a currency code.
* Orders go to suppliers by email with a PDF through <issue id="9aae6a50-4a43-4327-ae6a-51d18bc69b35" label="EVE-24" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-24/port-notifications-messaging-channels-and-transactional-email" />, plus a scoped supplier link to confirm. EDI and punchout are out of scope.

## Scope

1. Tables `purchasing_supplier_product` (supplier contact id, product, supplier sku, unit price minor units, currency, lead time days, min order qty), `purchasing_request` and lines (reason, event ref, status `draft`, `submitted`, `approved`, `rejected`), `purchasing_order` and lines (supplier, delivery location, expected date, tax code id per line, status `draft`, `sent`, `confirmed`, `part-received`, `received`, `cancelled`), `purchasing_receipt` and lines.
2. Actions `purchasing.supplierProduct.upsert`, `purchasing.request.create`, `approve` (confirm; approval limits per role from <issue id="9d08c369-bac9-4eb7-b8a7-329b5ec9bff0" label="EVE-41" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-41/p12-build-app-config-settings-secrets-and-credentials" /> settings), `reject`, `purchasing.order.create`, `update`, `send` (always needs approval; online only), `cancel` (always needs approval once sent), `purchasing.receipt.record`, `purchasing.reorder.evaluate`.
3. Receiving: `receipt.record` writes movements from the `supplier` location to the delivery location, registers serialised assets on receipt (F6.3, with cost from the order line), and emits one change-feed event per receipt `inventory:goods_received` with PO id, PO line ids, quantities, unit cost in minor units, currency, F1 Event id when present and supplier F4 Contact id, so F11 can three-way match and post goods received not invoiced at the time.
4. Reorder points per consumable product per warehouse (min, reorder qty); `reorder.evaluate` drafts requests grouped by preferred supplier.
5. Inbound open order lines add to availability from their expected date (F6.4 term).
6. Screens Suppliers and purchase orders, Goods receipt (scanner) with <issue id="91297309-acc4-4bb9-ad68-33fbc2720e5a" label="EVE-50" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-50/p111-build-screen-awareness-app-state-navigation-and-the-screen" /> projections and bound controls; supplier link page shows only that order.

## Acceptance criteria

* A request over the purchaser's limit needs finance approval; under it, the purchaser's confirm is enough.
* The agent drafting and sending an order stops for approval before `send`; after approval the supplier receives the email with PDF (test adapter).
* Receiving 2 of 5 lines marks the order `part-received`, writes movements, registers serialised assets with cost, and emits two `inventory:goods_received` events if done in two receipts.
* A consumable below its reorder point produces a draft request on `reorder.evaluate`, and the agent suggests it proactively (F6.12).
* Supplier prices are masked for roles without `inventory:valuation:read`.
