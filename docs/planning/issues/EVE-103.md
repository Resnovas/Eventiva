## Linear description

## Goal

Every quantity of every product has a place, and every change to stock is one recorded movement that people, agents and offline devices can add without conflicting.

## Scope

1. Tables `inventory_location` (tree: `warehouse`, `zone`, `bin`, `vehicle` referencing an F7 Vehicle, `event-site` referencing an F1 Event, `supplier`, `customer`, `repair`, `virtual` for adjustment, lost and scrap) and `inventory_movement` (product, optional asset, qty, from, to, reason, refs, actor person or agent, device, occurred_at, recorded_at). Movements are insert only; no update or delete path exists.
2. A derived `StockLevel` projection per product and location, rebuilt on device and server from movements; never written directly.
3. Actions `inventory.location.create`, `update`, `archive`; `inventory.stock.move`; `inventory.stock.adjust` (to or from the virtual adjustment location, approval confirm above an org threshold from <issue id="9d08c369-bac9-4eb7-b8a7-329b5ec9bff0" label="EVE-41" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-41/p12-build-app-config-settings-secrets-and-credentials" /> settings); `inventory.stock.levels` with an as-of time.
4. A negative level after merge raises a `negative-stock` Discrepancy (table owned by F6.6; until F6.6 lands, write a domain event `inventory:negative_stock`).
5. Domain events `inventory:stock_moved` and `inventory:stock_adjusted` on the change feed.
6. Screen Locations (Rambo-UI Tree and DataTable) with its <issue id="91297309-acc4-4bb9-ad68-33fbc2720e5a" label="EVE-50" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-50/p111-build-screen-awareness-app-state-navigation-and-the-screen" /> projection and bound controls.
7. Sync scopes: a device receives movements for the warehouses and events it is scoped to.

## Acceptance criteria

* Two offline devices each record 10 moves of the same bulk product; after reconnect both devices and the server show identical levels and 20 movements, with no conflict prompts.
* Replaying the same movement id twice stores it once.
* An adjustment above the threshold requires confirm when called by a person or by the agent (approval test).
* A level going negative after merge emits `inventory:negative_stock`.
* `inventory.stock.levels` as of a past time returns the level at that time.
* Archiving a location that holds stock fails with `LocationNotEmpty`.
