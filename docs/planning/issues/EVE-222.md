## Goal

Every sale produces a correct receipt, every payment is matched to the provider's payouts, organisers see live sales per event, channel and Space, and accounting receives the events it posts from.

## Scope

1. Receipts: template per org (logo, legal name, tax number, F11 tax code and amount per line, payment method, refund policy), delivered by email or SMS (<issue id="9aae6a50-4a43-4327-ae6a-51d18bc69b35" label="EVE-24" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-24/port-notifications-messaging-channels-and-transactional-email" />) or printed (F9.6); simplified VAT receipt on request; reprint.
2. sales.order.invoiceOnAccount hand-off to F11 and mark paid on accounting:invoice_settled.
3. Payout sync: sales.payout.list and get from the PaymentGateway; fees recorded per payment (sales:payment_fee_recorded).
4. Reconciliation: [sales.reconciliation.run](<http://sales.reconciliation.run>) matches orders, payments, refunds, payouts and cash sessions; reports unmatched, offline payments not forwarded within 24 hours, and cash variances. Nightly durable job (<issue id="a626308a-598f-41ba-accd-4004cbbd9932" label="EVE-54" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-54/p18c-build-durable-agent-runs-on-effect-cluster-and-workflow" />).
5. Reporting: sales.report.summary grouped by F1 Event, Phase, Space, channel, product, cashier and hour; sales dashboard with <issue id="1d65d374-8d06-4d69-a9c6-f3b66d565b47" label="EVE-30" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-30/port-data-widgets-saved-dashboards-and-brand-kits" /> widgets.
6. Timeline contribution of order and refund events to F4 contacts.
7. Event schemas published in the shared package and matched by a contract test that F11 also runs.

## Acceptance criteria

* Contract test: the five events validate against the shared schema; amounts sum to order totals in minor units.
* Reconciliation finds a seeded missing payout, an unforwarded offline payment and a cash variance.
* Dashboard numbers equal the report action's numbers for the same filters (the agent and the screen agree).
* A buyer's order appears on their F4 timeline.
