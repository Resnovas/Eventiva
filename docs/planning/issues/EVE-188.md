## Linear description

## Goal

When the company is short, it can hire equipment in from another supplier for the event dates, track it like its own stock, and send it back complete.

## Context

* Parent <issue id="928dc098-5cc9-4586-88bb-50abbe03071a" label="EVE-63" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-63/f6-warehousing-and-inventory-equipment-stock-availability-purchasing" /> sections 4, 5 and 7. Sub-hire is the most common answer to a shortage in the market check.
* Sub-hired items live in stock while held, from a `supplier` location to the warehouse or straight to the event-site, and must go back by the return date. Costs flow to F11 as a purchase (F6.8 order with a hire period).

## Scope

1. Table `inventory_subhire` and lines (supplier F4 Contact id, event, window collect and return, product qty or supplier serials, purchase order ref, status `draft`, `sent`, `confirmed`, `received`, `returned`, `cancelled`).
2. Actions `inventory.subhire.create` (from a shortage, prefilled), `send` (always needs approval; creates or sends the linked F6.8 purchase order with the hire period), `receive` (movements in; serialised items get temporary asset records flagged `subhired`, with the supplier's tag codes), `return` (movements out; missing items raise discrepancies).
3. Availability (F6.4) adds confirmed sub-hire lines for their window only; sub-hired assets are never offered beyond the window.
4. Agent: from `shortage.list`, proposes a sub-hire with the preferred supplier (lowest price then shortest lead time from F6.8 supplier products) and a draft order, then stops for approval.
5. Screen Sub-hire with <issue id="91297309-acc4-4bb9-ad68-33fbc2720e5a" label="EVE-50" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-50/p111-build-screen-awareness-app-state-navigation-and-the-screen" /> projection and bound controls; the Event equipment tab shows sub-hired lines with a badge.

## Acceptance criteria

* A shortage of 4 moving heads becomes a sub-hire for the event window; after approval and receipt, availability for that window shows no shortage and outside the window is unchanged.
* Returning 3 of 4 raises one `missing` discrepancy against the sub-hire.
* "Hire in what we are missing for Saturday" run by the agent ends at an approval prompt with the supplier, items, window and cost shown.
* Temporary asset records are retired automatically after return.
