## Linear description

## Goal

Workers see a correct leave balance and book time off from any device, managers approve with roster clashes in front of them, approved leave blocks availability for rostering, and payroll gets the absence it needs.

## Scope

1. Tables LeavePolicy (versioned: leaveType, jurisdictionRule, accrual method fixed, pro rata or per hours worked, carry-over, year start), policy assignments, LeaveLedgerEntry (append-only: accrual, taken, adjustment, carry-over, expiry), LeaveRequest, AbsenceRecord.
2. Balance is always the ledger sum as of a date (pure function, plain TypeScript); no stored balance column.
3. UK rule pack behind a `LeaveJurisdictionRule` port: 5.6 weeks statutory minimum pro rata for part-time; irregular-hours and part-year accrual at 12.07 percent of hours worked for leave years starting on or after 1 April 2024; bank holidays per policy (included or on top). Other jurisdictions use configurable policies only.
4. `PublicHolidays` port with a cached public open-data feed per region (no account needed); `hr.publicHolidays.sync`.
5. Actions `hr.leavePolicies.*`, `hr.leave.balance`, `.request`, `.approve`, `.reject`, `.cancel`, `.adjust`, `hr.absence.record`, `hr.leave.calendar`, per <issue id="253839bc-c987-416b-92ea-206c9e9e8d2e" label="EVE-80" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-80/f13-human-resources-worker-records-onboarding-qualifications-and-leave" /> section 4.
6. Request creation works offline; approval is online and re-checks balance, overlapping requests and clashes with F2 roster assignments and F1 Event dates; approval writes a taken ledger entry and an <issue id="97915f03-fb56-4efc-bb5a-15e8b62b96b2" label="EVE-33" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-33/port-scheduling-primitives-for-availability-bookings-and-rosters" /> availability block in one transaction; cancel reverses both.
7. Approval routing to the line manager (F13.1 reporting lines) with fallback to HR; optional tenant auto-approve rules (for example under one day with no clash).
8. Self-reported sickness through `hr.absence.record`, fit note upload via <issue id="8d8d301b-d679-4dac-95bb-c24889179cd0" label="EVE-25" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-25/port-files-private-attachments-artifacts-and-document-import" />.
9. `hr.leave` and `me.leave` screens with projections and bound controls per <issue id="253839bc-c987-416b-92ea-206c9e9e8d2e" label="EVE-80" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-80/f13-human-resources-worker-records-onboarding-qualifications-and-leave" /> section 6.

## Acceptance criteria

* A full-time worker on a UK policy with an April year start has 28 days including bank holidays on day one of the year; an irregular-hours worker who works 100 hours accrues 12.07 hours.
* A request made offline syncs, and on approval the balance drops, the ledger has one taken entry and <issue id="97915f03-fb56-4efc-bb5a-15e8b62b96b2" label="EVE-33" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-33/port-scheduling-primitives-for-availability-bookings-and-rosters" /> rejects a booking in the leave period.
* Approving leave that clashes with an existing F2 roster assignment shows the clash to the approver and still needs explicit approval.
* The agent asked "book me Friday off" creates a request, never approves its own user's request, and reports the pending status.
* Cancelling approved leave restores the balance and removes the availability block.
