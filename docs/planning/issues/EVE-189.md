## Linear description

## Goal

The warehouse can count a bin, zone or whole site, on several devices at once and offline, and turn the differences into reviewed adjustments.

## Context

* Parent <issue id="928dc098-5cc9-4586-88bb-50abbe03071a" label="EVE-63" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-63/f6-warehousing-and-inventory-equipment-stock-availability-purchasing" /> sections 4 and 5. Counts compare a snapshot of derived levels (F6.2) with what was counted; differences become adjustment movements only on finalise.
* Movements that happen during a count (for example a late return) must not be counted as differences.

## Scope

1. Tables `inventory_count` (scope location, mode `full` or `cycle`, snapshot time, status `open`, `review`, `finalised`) and `inventory_count_line` (product or asset, counted qty, device, counter person).
2. Actions `inventory.count.start` (takes the snapshot), `record` (per device; offline allowed), `finalise` (online only, confirm; writes adjustment movements to or from the virtual adjustment location and raises discrepancies for serialised assets not found).
3. Difference = counted minus (snapshot level plus movements into or out of the scope after the snapshot).
4. Cycle counts: a schedule per location (from <issue id="9d08c369-bac9-4eb7-b8a7-329b5ec9bff0" label="EVE-41" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-41/p12-build-app-config-settings-secrets-and-credentials" /> settings) and a read listing locations due; the agent suggests the next count.
5. Screen Stock count (scanner list) with <issue id="91297309-acc4-4bb9-ad68-33fbc2720e5a" label="EVE-50" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-50/p111-build-screen-awareness-app-state-navigation-and-the-screen" /> projection and bound controls.

## Acceptance criteria

* Two devices count the same zone offline; counts sum per line after sync with no double counting of the same serialised asset.
* A return scanned in after the snapshot does not show as a difference.
* Finalise writes one adjustment per differing bulk line and one `missing` discrepancy per serialised asset not counted, and needs confirm for person and agent alike.
