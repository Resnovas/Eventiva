Status: Approved (2026-11-08). Jonathan chose decision D3 (b): F8 builds its own seat map editor now instead of waiting for F10 site maps.

## Goal

An organiser draws a seating plan for a Space in F8 (sections, rows, seats, tables, standing areas, accessible spaces), sells specific seats, and buyers pick seats from the same plan, with no seat sold twice.

## Scope

1. SeatPlan document: Space ref, sections, rows (straight, curved), seats (label, row, number, accessibility tags, price zone), tables with seats, general-admission areas with capacity, aisles and labels. Versioned; a plan in use is locked to a published version.
2. Editor screen: add a section, generate rows and seats in bulk (count, spacing, curve, numbering scheme), select and edit many seats, assign price zones to ticket types, mark accessible and companion seats, mark seats held or killed (sight-line, production). Every control bound to an action; projection lists the selection, the section and counts.
3. Actions: ticketing.seatPlan.create, update (patch ops), publish, duplicate; ticketing.seatPlan.generateRows; ticketing.seatPlan.assignZone; ticketing.seatPlan.setSeatState (held, killed, open). Agent approval: confirm for publish and killing seats on sale.
4. Seat inventory from the published plan into the F8.2 pool entity (per-seat state next to the counter); best-available and pick-your-seat modes; editing a published plan cannot remove or move a sold seat (typed error).
5. [Ticket.seat](<http://Ticket.seat>) ref set on issue; seat shown on ticket, pass and scanner.
6. Buyer seat picker (read-only render of the plan with seat states) on the public ticket picker and box office.

## Acceptance criteria

* An organiser builds a 2,000-seat plan with 4 sections in under 10 minutes using bulk row generation (timed usability test).
* The agent, asked "add 10 rows of 20 seats behind section A, last row wheelchair spaces", produces the change and asks before publishing.
* Two buyers picking the same seat at once: one succeeds, the other gets SeatTaken.
* Removing a sold seat from the published plan fails with SeatSold.
* Two editors and the agent editing the plan concurrently, one offline, converge after reconnect.
* A 5,000-seat plan renders and is pickable on a mid-range Android device in under 2 seconds; editor and picker meet WCAG 2.2 AA, including a list view of seats for screen readers.

## Constraints

Effect-TS v3 house rules; Rambo-UI components; rendering through F10's MapEngine port only (MapLibre, approved via D-F10-1 in <issue id="f8432c08-24bb-4690-a3e1-16ed79977eee" label="EVE-108" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-108/f10-interactive-site-maps" />); no other canvas or rendering library; behind flags `ticketing` and `ticketing-seating`; ships to main per the AGENTS.md delivery exception with `node --run check` results pasted here.
