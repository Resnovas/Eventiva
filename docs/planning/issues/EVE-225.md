## Goal

A production company quotes a client for equipment hire, services and fees for an event, the client accepts online and pays a deposit, the hire is reserved for its dates, and the balance is paid or invoiced before the event.

## Scope

1. Quote entity and state machine: draft, sent, accepted, declined, expired; revisions (a sent quote is immutable; revise creates revision n+1).
2. Hire pricing: per day, per week or per event, with day multipliers (for example 1 day, 3 days priced as 2), minimum charge; hire lines carry a period; availability check through F6 before sending, and a reservation on accept.
3. Actions: sales.quote.create, revise, send (confirm), accept (by the customer through a signed link or by staff), decline; accept creates an Order with the quote's lines.
4. Deposits: deposit percent or amount on the quote; accept takes the deposit online (F9.3) or hands the order to F11 on account; balance due date; balance reminder (<issue id="9aae6a50-4a43-4327-ae6a-51d18bc69b35" label="EVE-24" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-24/port-notifications-messaging-channels-and-transactional-email" />); balance by card or invoice.
5. Quote PDF and email to the client (<issue id="9aae6a50-4a43-4327-ae6a-51d18bc69b35" label="EVE-24" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-24/port-notifications-messaging-channels-and-transactional-email" />, <issue id="8d8d301b-d679-4dac-95bb-c24889179cd0" label="EVE-25" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-25/port-files-private-attachments-artifacts-and-document-import" />), whitelabel.
6. Hire returns and damage charges: F6 records the check-in; damage or late return adds a charge line to the order through sales.order.addCharge (approval confirm).
7. Quotes list and Quote editor screens with projections; the agent can draft a quote from an enquiry for a person to send.

## Acceptance criteria

* A quote for 3 days of hire plus a crew service is sent, accepted through the link, a 30 percent deposit is paid with the fake gateway, F6 reserves the equipment for the period, and the balance reminder is scheduled.
* Accepting after expiry fails with a typed error; a revised quote can be accepted.
* A late return adds a charge line after confirmation; F11 receives the event.
