## Goal

Crew on a muddy field with no signal open the map for their role, find their post and the nearest facility, and keep working; everything syncs when signal returns.

## Scope

1. `sitemap.offline.preparePack`: manifest for a map covering site bbox plus a margin, zoom range (default 14 to 20; the OpenStreetMap basemap extract stops at zoom 15 and is overzoomed above it), a per-site basemap PMTiles extract, imagery only where the licence allows (D-F10-6; by default imagery is online only and drone or survey orthophotos uploaded by the tenant stand in offline), calibrated base layers, the layer documents in the user's audience, symbols and the search index slice; size estimate before download.
2. Automatic pack download for rostered crew 48 hours before their first shift (setting), on Wi-Fi only by default.
3. Crew map screen (phone first): my layers, my post from F2, "near me" facilities by category, search by label or symbol; offline banner with pack age.
4. `sitemap.find` works offline against the local index and online against <issue id="2cd1c920-450c-487e-b4c8-e860a382a6b0" label="EVE-26" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-26/port-search-and-embeddings-across-every-resource" />.
5. Pack updates: when a new revision or layer change lands, download the delta (Yjs updates and changed tiles only).
6. Storage limits: warn above 500 MB, let the user remove packs.

## Acceptance criteria

* On Android in airplane mode after a cold restart: the map, layers and search work from the pack.
* An edit made offline syncs on reconnect and converges (<issue id="a1348819-43cc-4005-8e8d-a896a761af03" label="EVE-51" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-51/p112-build-collaboration-yjs-for-text-and-canvas-with-record-sync-and" /> path).
* A role change that removes a layer removes it from the pack on next sync.
* A delta update after a small edit downloads under 50 KB.
