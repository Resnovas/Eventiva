## Goal

Everything a plan needs (fixtures, truss, speakers, cable, risers) becomes a list of equipment lines per phase and department that warehousing reserves, the marketplace can source, and accounting can cost.

## Scope

1. Table `equipment_requirement` (event_id, phase_id optional, department_id, catalogue_ref to F6 product id, quantity, needed_from, needed_to, source: rollup or manual, source_element_ids, fulfilment: unfilled, reserved, hired, notes).
2. Pure roll-up function: groups DesignElements by department, catalogue_ref and phase window; adds spares by a per-department spare percentage from the org configuration schema (default 0); never deletes manual lines; marks rollup lines whose elements were removed as `stale` instead of deleting them.
3. Actions: `requirement.rollUp` (idempotent), `requirement.update`, `requirement.addManual`, `requirement.remove`, `requirement.sendToWarehouse` (confirm; emits `requirement.sent` on the change feed for F6), `requirement.setFulfilment` (called by F6 and F5 only, permission `f1:requirement:fulfil`).
4. Elements with no `catalogue_ref` are reported, not guessed; the agent may suggest a match from the F6 catalogue for confirmation.
5. Screen: Requirements tab in the Department workspace (grouped list, stale and unmatched markers, fulfilment status, send button) with projection and bound controls.

## Acceptance criteria

* Placing 24 of one fixture and 6 truss sections and rolling up gives two lines with the right quantities; rolling up again changes nothing.
* Removing 4 fixtures and rolling up updates the quantity; a manual line is untouched.
* `requirement.sendToWarehouse` produces one change-feed event F6 can consume (contract test with a fake consumer).
* Only F6 and F5 service identities can call `requirement.setFulfilment`.
* Screen passes the unbound-control check and axe.

### Planning decision - exact versus functional requirement policy (2026-11-13; coordinate D5-13 in <issue id="f08bafe4-f32d-4906-9e87-5d69df56d6a0" label="EVE-124" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-124/f58-build-quotes-quote-comparison-and-the-acceptance-handoff-to-the" />)

* Each versioned EquipmentRequirement has a line-level procurement/fulfilment policy: **exact specified manufacturer/model/variant** where mandated by a performer rider or technical requirement, or **functional/equivalent permitted** when the organiser specifies an outcome rather than a brand (e.g. one handheld microphone for spoken-word use, or one lapel microphone). Tenant/category defaults may prefill but explicit per-line settings prevail.
* Exact requirements record specific model/variant, quantity, rider provenance and hard constraints. Functional requirements record usage/outcome and required hard/soft acceptance criteria (type/form factor, wiring/connectivity, compatible accessories, RF/environment/safety/performance where relevant), even when no model is prescribed.
* Supplier-branded candidate products and evaluated equivalence are **proposals linked to the requirement ID/version**, not replacements of the F1 source record. An alternate to an exact rider is a visible non-compliant proposal pending separately authorised waiver, never automatically equivalent.
* A user may explicitly adopt a supplier's proposed alternative to the event plan, creating a new version with change-impact propagation and existing manual supplier-release approval. Merely choosing an option for quote review or grouping alternatives does not rewrite a plan or executed commitment.
* Verify: one event carries an exact rider mic and a generic spoken-word handheld concurrently; different branded candidates match the second by verified constraints, not the first; changes and exceptions are recorded and auditable. Do not force an exact catalogue SKU for a function-based line.
