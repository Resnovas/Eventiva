## Linear description

## Goal

A production manager, a sales channel or the agent can see what is free for a date range and hold it for an event or a cart, and shortages appear the moment they happen, including after offline edits sync.

## Context

F1 names are fixed in <issue id="fbcff205-decd-464a-b8ab-a1278e71fbe4" label="EVE-73" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-73/f1-events-and-production-planning-the-event-record-run-of-show" /> section 5: Reservation attaches to an `Event` and optionally a `Phase`; event-site locations reference the `Event` or a `Space`.

* F9 contract (<issue id="928dc098-5cc9-4586-88bb-50abbe03071a" label="EVE-63" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-63/f6-warehousing-and-inventory-equipment-stock-availability-purchasing" /> section 8): line kind `stock` calls `reservation.create` on cart add (with expiry), `commit` on payment, `release` on expiry, cancel or refund with restock.

## Scope

1. Tables `inventory_reservation` (event, phase, window out and return due, status `hold`, `confirmed`, `picking`, `out`, `returned`, `cancelled`, `conflict`, source `manual`, `plan`, `sales`, expiry for sales holds) and `inventory_reservation_line` (product qty or specific asset, requirement ref).
2. Availability per product per day: owned stock in the warehouse minus confirmed and held reservations overlapping the window, minus quarantined and retired assets, plus inbound purchase receipts and sub-hires due before the window (F6.8 and F6.9 add these terms when they land). Kits expand to components.
3. Actions `inventory.availability.query`, `inventory.reservation.create`, `update`, `cancel`, `fromPlan`, `commit`, `release`, `inventory.shortage.list` (each shortage with options: sub-hire, purchase, substitute product in the same category, transfer from another warehouse).
4. Server re-check on sync: when a reservation made offline overbooks, its lines take status `conflict`, a `inventory:shortage_detected` event is emitted, and nothing is dropped.
5. Sales holds expire automatically (Effect schedule on the server) and emit `inventory:reservation_released`.
6. Screens Availability calendar and Event equipment (a tab on the F1 Event screen) with <issue id="91297309-acc4-4bb9-ad68-33fbc2720e5a" label="EVE-50" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-50/p111-build-screen-awareness-app-state-navigation-and-the-screen" /> projections and bound controls.

## Acceptance criteria

* With 10 owned units, holds of 6 and 5 overlapping on one day give availability -1 and one shortage listing all four option types.
* `fromPlan` on an event with 3 EquipmentRequirement rows across 2 phases creates one draft reservation with 3 lines and windows from needed_from and needed_to; re-running it updates rather than duplicates.
* Two offline devices each reserve the last 4 units for different events; after sync one is `confirmed`, the later one is `conflict`, and a shortage event exists.
* A sales hold not committed before expiry is released and availability returns.
* `commit` writes movements to the customer location for a sale; `release` with restock writes the reverse movement.
* The agent answers "what am I short for Saturday" from `shortage.list`.
