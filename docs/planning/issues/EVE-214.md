## Linear description

## Goal

Every paid order line reaches the customer by the method it was sold with (ship, click and collect, collect at the event, digital delivery), and staff can see and work the queue, including offline hand over at an event.

## Context

* Parent <issue id="7d939472-20ba-4512-b231-c0f04c05574c" label="EVE-107" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-107/f9-online-and-in-person-sales-storefront-point-of-sale-orders-payments" /> sections 4, 5 and 6. Stock commit happens in F9.2 through F6 (<issue id="928dc098-5cc9-4586-88bb-50abbe03071a" label="EVE-63" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-63/f6-warehousing-and-inventory-equipment-stock-availability-purchasing" />); this slice moves goods to the customer, not stock levels.
* Tickets are fulfilled by F8 (ticketing.ticket.issueForOrder), not here.

## Scope

1. Fulfilment methods per channel and product: ship (address, manual tracking number), click and collect (pickup location), collect at event (F1 Event plus Space, with a pickup code), digital (file from <issue id="8d8d301b-d679-4dac-95bb-c24889179cd0" label="EVE-25" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-25/port-files-private-attachments-artifacts-and-document-import" /> or a link, delivered by email), none (fees, donations).
2. Fulfilment state machine: pending, packed, shipped or ready for collection, handed over or delivered, returned.
3. Actions: sales.fulfilment.create, pack, ship, handOver (by pickup code or order search), deliverDigital.
4. `ShippingCarrier` port defined (rates, labels, tracking) with a manual adapter only; no carrier vendor in this slice.
5. Fulfilment queue screen (board by status, scan input for pickup codes) and fulfilment section in Order detail.
6. Offline hand over at an event: pickup codes and pending orders for that event cached on the device; hand over recorded offline and synced append-only; a code used twice offline is flagged on sync.
7. Customer emails for shipped, ready for collection and digital delivery through <issue id="9aae6a50-4a43-4327-ae6a-51d18bc69b35" label="EVE-24" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-24/port-notifications-messaging-channels-and-transactional-email" />.

## Acceptance criteria

* One order with a shipped line, a collect-at-event line and a digital line moves each line through its states; customer emails sent with the fake notifier.
* Offline hand over of 20 pickup codes syncs once each; a duplicate scan on two devices is flagged, not silently accepted twice.
* Digital link is private to the customer and expires per setting.

## Constraints

Effect-TS v3 house rules; Rambo-UI components; behind the `f9-sales` flag; no carrier vendor or new dependency without Jonathan approving it on this issue; ships to main per the AGENTS.md delivery exception with `node --run check` results pasted here.
