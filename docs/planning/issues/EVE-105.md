## Linear description

## Goal

Eventiva knows, for every person, what they agreed to and on what legal basis, blocks any send they did not agree to, and lets a tenant answer access and erasure requests on time.

## Context

* Parent <issue id="ad791a38-f528-4aec-9433-c36977bbe8e2" label="EVE-65" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-65/f4-contacts-and-crm-people-organisations-relationships-and-history" /> sections 5 (ConsentRecord, PrivacyRequest), 11 (compliance: UK and EU GDPR, PECR, CAN-SPAM, CCPA/CPRA) and 7 (erasure always needs human approval).
* Sends go through <issue id="9aae6a50-4a43-4327-ae6a-51d18bc69b35" label="EVE-24" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-24/port-notifications-messaging-channels-and-transactional-email" />; this issue adds a consent check that <issue id="9aae6a50-4a43-4327-ae6a-51d18bc69b35" label="EVE-24" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-24/port-notifications-messaging-channels-and-transactional-email" /> calls before any marketing send.
* Erasure must reach records other sets own, through the `PartyReferenceOwner` contract from F4.4, and keep legally required finance records (F11) as tombstones.

## Scope

1. Append-only `consent_record` (purpose, channel, lawful basis, state, source, captured at, evidence) and `privacy_request` (kind, status, due at, fulfilled by, result file).
2. Actions `crm.consent.record` / `withdraw` / `get`, `crm.consent.check` (purpose, channel, party; used by senders), `crm.privacy.requestExport` / `requestErasure` / `requestRectification` / `fulfil` (`always` approval for erasure) / `list`.
3. Export bundle: every record about the party across registered owners, as JSON plus a readable summary, stored via <issue id="8d8d301b-d679-4dac-95bb-c24889179cd0" label="EVE-25" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-25/port-files-private-attachments-artifacts-and-document-import" />.
4. Erasure job (<issue id="a626308a-598f-41ba-accd-4004cbbd9932" label="EVE-54" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-54/p18c-build-durable-agent-runs-on-effect-cluster-and-workflow" />): removes personal fields, replaces with a tombstone where a set declares a legal hold, writes one audit row per owner touched.
5. Due-date tracking (one month from receipt by default, tenant setting) with a notification at 7 days left (<issue id="9aae6a50-4a43-4327-ae6a-51d18bc69b35" label="EVE-24" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-24/port-notifications-messaging-channels-and-transactional-email" />).
6. Tenant retention policy settings (defaults in the parent, section 11) and a scheduled purge of archived parties past retention.
7. Screens: Consent tab on Person detail, Consent and privacy admin page.

## Acceptance criteria

* Tests: a withdrawal recorded offline and synced before a queued send blocks that send; erasure leaves no personal field in any registered owner except legal holds; an erasure cannot run without a human approval even when the agent requests it; export contains records from at least two owners.
* `node --run check` green.

## Constraints

Effect-TS v3; no personal data in telemetry; legal wording reviewed by Jonathan before customer release; no new dependency without approval here; ship to main with check results.

## Done signal

Merged with tests green and a compliance note in the people docs listing what is and is not covered.
