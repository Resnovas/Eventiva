## Goal

Box office staff sell, print or send tickets at the door through F9 point of sale, look up and reissue tickets, and keep selling from a fixed per-device quota when offline without any risk of overselling.

## Context

* Parent <issue id="8bd88b8d-9173-4f07-a92d-1c317836f4b2" label="EVE-76" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-76/f8-ticketing-ticket-types-capacity-issuance-admissions-and-scanning" /> sections 4 and 5; F9 thread agreed (2026-11-01) that kind "ticket" is not online-only and uses a pre-allocated per-device allocation from a capacity pool for offline POS.
* F9 owns the POS cart, payment and receipt; F8 owns the holds, ticket issue and print or send.

## Scope

1. Box office screen: ticket type grid with live availability, F9 cart, holder form, print, email or show-on-screen delivery, lookup and reissue.
2. ticketing.boxOffice.allocateOfflineQuota: reserves a hold (purpose offline-quota) for a device and ticket type; returning the device or ending the shift releases the unused part.
3. Offline sale: device issues provisional tickets from its quota, signed with a per-device sub-key certified by the event key (so scanners verify them offline); on reconnect F9 posts the orders and F8 converts provisional tickets to normal ones keeping the same code.
4. Print through the OS print path (PDF from <issue id="8d8d301b-d679-4dac-95bb-c24889179cd0" label="EVE-25" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-25/port-files-private-attachments-artifacts-and-document-import" />); native printer SDKs only on approval.
5. Shift summary: sold, comps, reissues, quota left, handed to F9 for cash-up.

## Acceptance criteria

* An offline device with a quota of 50 refuses the 51st sale with QuotaExhausted.
* Provisional tickets sold offline admit at an offline scanner and stay valid after sync with the same code.
* Ending a shift releases unused quota back to the pool within 5 seconds of reconnect.
* Sales made offline reconcile exactly with F9 orders (count and value) in an integration test.
* Agent can prepare a quota allocation but needs confirmation; it cannot sell at the box office.

## Constraints

Effect-TS v3 house rules; device sub-keys only from the secrets service (<issue id="9d08c369-bac9-4eb7-b8a7-329b5ec9bff0" label="EVE-41" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-41/p12-build-app-config-settings-secrets-and-credentials" />), revocable; behind flags `ticketing` and `ticketing-box-office`; no new dependency without Jonathan approving it here; ships to main per the AGENTS.md delivery exception with `node --run check` results pasted here.
