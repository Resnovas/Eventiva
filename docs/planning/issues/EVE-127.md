## Goal

Supplier bills and crew expenses reach the books with a receipt, the right event and an approval, and the agent does the typing.

## Context

* Parent [EVE-69](<https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-69/f11-accounting-ledgers-invoices-bills-event-budgets-and-tax>) sections 4 to 7. Suppliers are F4 Parties (<issue id="ad791a38-f528-4aec-9433-c36977bbe8e2" label="EVE-65" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-65/f4-contacts-and-crm-people-organisations-relationships-and-history" />), covered by the same PartyReferenceOwner registration and legal hold as F11.4; claimants are F13 staff members. ## Scope

1. `Bill`, `ExpenseClaim`, `DocumentLine`; states draft, submitted, approved, rejected, scheduled, paid (<issue id="e4fa521d-c5d2-4f24-ab86-ed41def53953" label="EVE-42" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-42/p13-build-the-storage-layer-drizzle-on-postgres-and-sqlite-state" />). Drafts and claims work offline, including the photo.
2. Three-way match: a bill line can link to F6 PO lines and goods receipts; price or quantity differences beyond a tenant tolerance (default 2 percent) block approval with a typed reason.
3. Approval policies: by amount bands, by F1 Event (event manager approves their events), by department; the drafter can never approve their own bill or claim.
4. Actions: `accounting.bill.draft` / `update` / `submit` / `approve` / `reject`, `accounting.bill.extract` (proposal only), `accounting.expense.submit` / `approve` / `reject`, `accounting.payable.aged`, `accounting.payment.batch.prepare` (bank upload file, UK Bacs standard 18 CSV or the bank's CSV; no payment initiation in this slice).
5. On approval: post payable, expense or asset, VAT, and clear GRNI where matched.
6. Screens: bills list and editor with file viewer, expense capture (mobile first, camera), approvals inbox. Projections and bound controls.
7. Agent: from an uploaded or emailed receipt, propose supplier, date, lines, tax and F1 Event with confidence; never approves.

## Acceptance criteria

* A claim captured in airplane mode with a photo syncs on reconnect and appears in the right approver's inbox.
* A bill 5 percent above its PO price is blocked; within 2 percent it passes.
* The drafter approving their own bill is denied on every surface (UI, HTTP, MCP, ACP).
* Extraction on a fixture set of 20 receipts fills supplier, date, total and VAT correctly on at least 18 (evaluation tracked in <issue id="bef04957-8bec-479e-85ee-5880dfc9fc8c" label="EVE-29" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-29/port-agent-evaluations-linked-to-posthog-datasets" /> when it exists).
