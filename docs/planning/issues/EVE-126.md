## Linear description

## Goal

A finance user or the agent drafts an invoice from an F1 Event or an F9 order, issues it with a gapless number and a VAT-compliant PDF, sends it, and sees it paid off in receivables.

## Context

* Parent [EVE-69](<https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-69/f11-accounting-ledgers-invoices-bills-event-budgets-and-tax>) sections 4 to 7 and 11. Customers are F4 Parties (Organisation or Person, <issue id="ad791a38-f528-4aec-9433-c36977bbe8e2" label="EVE-65" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-65/f4-contacts-and-crm-people-organisations-relationships-and-history" />); invoices copy legal name, address and tax number from the Party at issue so the issued document never changes. F11 registers as a PartyReferenceOwner (<issue id="7692d04c-3444-4989-b2aa-b73a1a69ab02" label="EVE-99" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-99/f44-find-and-merge-duplicate-contacts-and-import-and-export-them" />) and holds a legal hold against erasure (<issue id="4b6f8805-b1b2-40f7-99e9-089c9adb8002" label="EVE-105" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-105/f48-record-consent-and-fulfil-privacy-requests-for-contacts" />). F9 hands pay-on-account orders to F11 with `sales.order.invoiceOnAccount` and waits for `accounting:invoice_settled` on the change feed (agreed with F9, 2026-11-01).
* Tax codes from F11.7; until F11.7 lands, a seeded UK standard, reduced, zero and exempt set is used.
* Files and PDFs from [EVE-25](<https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-25/port-files-private-attachments-artifacts-and-document-import>); email from [EVE-24](<https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-24/port-notifications-messaging-channels-and-transactional-email>); the customer link is served through [EVE-46](<https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-46/p17-build-the-http-api-streaming-and-the-typed-client-from-the-action>).

## Scope

1. `Invoice`, `CreditNote`, `DocumentLine`, `TaxLine`; states draft, issued, part paid, paid, void (<issue id="e4fa521d-c5d2-4f24-ab86-ed41def53953" label="EVE-42" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-42/p13-build-the-storage-layer-drizzle-on-postgres-and-sqlite-state" /> state machine). Drafts sync offline; issue is online only.
2. Numbering sequences per tenant (prefix, next number), assigned in the issue transaction; never reused, never gapped.
3. Actions: `accounting.invoice.draft` / `update` / `issue` / `send` / `remind` / `void`, `accounting.creditNote.issue`, `accounting.invoice.fromEvent` (lines from F1 Event quote items when F1 defines them), `accounting.invoice.fromOrder` (the F9 hand-off), `accounting.invoice.view` (token-scoped for customers), `accounting.receivable.statement`.
4. On issue: post receivable and revenue and VAT through F11.1; render PDF with all UK VAT invoice fields; store via <issue id="8d8d301b-d679-4dac-95bb-c24889179cd0" label="EVE-25" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-25/port-files-private-attachments-artifacts-and-document-import" />.
5. On settlement (payment allocations from F11.6 or F9 payments): emit `accounting:invoice_settled`.
6. Screens: invoices list, invoice editor with PDF preview, customer invoice view (external link). Projections and bound controls.
7. Agent: draft invoices on request; list overdue invoices daily and propose reminders (sending needs approval unless a standing rule exists).

## Acceptance criteria

* 1,000 concurrent issues across two runners produce 1,000 distinct consecutive numbers (concurrency test).
* An issued invoice cannot change; a credit note reverses it fully or partly and posts the reversal.
* The PDF includes every field on the HMRC VAT invoice checklist (snapshot test against a fixture list).
* The customer link shows only that invoice; a tampered token is denied.
* An F9 pay-on-account order becomes an invoice and F9 marks it paid after settlement (contract test with an F9 fixture).

## Constraints

Effect-TS v3; no new dependency (PDF rendering uses what <issue id="8d8d301b-d679-4dac-95bb-c24889179cd0" label="EVE-25" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-25/port-files-private-attachments-artifacts-and-document-import" /> provides, or asks here first); customer-facing text whitelabel and ASCII; flag `f11-accounting`; ship to main with `node --run check` results pasted here.
