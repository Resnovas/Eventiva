## Linear description

## Goal

Organisers and vendors sell time-based services (a workshop slot, a meet and greet, a production service, an hour of a crew role) where the customer picks a time, the slot is held during checkout and confirmed when paid.

## Context

* Parent <issue id="7d939472-20ba-4512-b231-c0f04c05574c" label="EVE-107" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-107/f9-online-and-in-person-sales-storefront-point-of-sale-orders-payments" /> sections 4 and 5. Availability and bookings are F2 scheduling, built on <issue id="97915f03-fb56-4efc-bb5a-15e8b62b96b2" label="EVE-33" href="https://linear.app/resnovas/issue/EVE-33/port-scheduling-primitives-for-availability-bookings-and-rosters" />. F9 implements nothing about availability; it registers line kind `service` provided by F2.

## Scope

1. Agree with the F2 thread the three provider actions behind kind `service` (reserve a slot with expiry, confirm booking, cancel booking) and record their names here before coding.
2. Product kind `service` with duration, capacity per slot, resource or role link (F2), and price per slot or per hour.
3. Slot picker in the storefront and register (reads F2 availability), cart line with the slot as the reference id and the hold as the reservation id.
4. Paid order confirms the booking; refund or cancel cancels it per policy; reschedule as an exchange (F9.8).
5. Service lines are `online-only` offline mode unless F2 provides per-device allocation.

## Acceptance criteria

* A customer books and pays for a 30 minute slot; a second customer cannot take the same slot while it is held; expiry frees it.
* Cancelling the order cancels the booking in F2.
* Offline register blocks service lines with a clear message.
